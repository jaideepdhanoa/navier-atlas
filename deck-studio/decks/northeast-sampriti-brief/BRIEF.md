# Navier × the Northeast

*Briefing note · July 2026 · Prepared by Navier for introduction*

**In one line:** Navier builds electric hydrofoil water taxis in Alameda, California — boats that rise out of the water and fly above it, cutting energy use by up to 90%* — and New York and New England are the best waters in America to put them to work. We are seeking introductions to the region's transportation decision-makers, beginning with NYC EDC.

## 1 · What Navier is

Navier is a California boatbuilder founded by an MIT, NASA and GoogleX team. Our vessel, the N30, is an eight-passenger electric hydrofoil — a carbon-fibre boat that lifts clear of the water and flies silently above it. No wake, no noise, no emissions, and a smoother ride than any conventional ferry in chop.

This is not a concept. The N30 is signed, funded and operating today:

- **Signed — a $100M national network.** A 100-vessel national ferry network for the Maldives, on this same platform.
- **Deployed — the U.S. Navy.** Deployed with the U.S. Navy and inside Leidos programs — proven beyond the demo dock.
- **A family of vessels.** The 8-seat N30 flying today; a 20-seat N45; and a long-range 8-seat variant built for corridors like Manhattan ↔ the Hamptons.

## 2 · Why New York, and why now

- **Congestion pricing is working — and the water is the uncongested lane.** In its first year the Congestion Relief Zone cut vehicle entries by 27 million (11%), and transit use rose across subway and bus. The harbor is the one right-of-way in the region with no traffic on it. *(MTA one-year report, 2026)*
- **LaGuardia still has no rail link.** The AirTrain LGA project was cancelled in March 2023, and the Port Authority set aside $30M to study alternatives. The Marine Air Terminal sits directly on the water — minutes from Midtown by foil. *(PANYNJ, 2023)*
- **NYC Ferry is at record demand, on diesel.** More than 7.1 million boardings in FY24 — a record — on a conventional diesel catamaran fleet, at a $9.09 per-passenger operating subsidy. The City has said it will begin a study of transition to zero-emission vessels. *(NYCEDC NYC Ferry FY24 Annual Report)*
- **The East End's only fast link to Manhattan is by air.** Blade reported 94,733 short-distance seats flown and $72.2M in short-distance revenue in FY2024 (company-wide). A foiling vessel offers dock-to-dock, all-weather service at a fraction of the cost per seat. *(Blade Air Mobility 2024 Form 10-K)*
- **New England is actively planning for exactly this.** MBTA's ferry operator reports more than 1.4 million passengers carried in 2024; the MassDOT–MBTA Water Transportation Study runs through summer 2026; Massport has accelerated its Net Zero target to 2028; and the Steamship Authority — 2.96 million passengers in 2025 — has studied alternative propulsion and secured $28.1M in federal funding for fleet conversion. *(MBTA · Massport · SSA 2025 Annual Report)*
