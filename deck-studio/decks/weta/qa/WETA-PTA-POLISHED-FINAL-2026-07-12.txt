 HOW IT WORKS

 One network, three roles for right-sized vessels


 STRENGTHEN EXISTING SERVICE
 Add targeted frequency or a smaller capacity increment where WETA identifies a real rider need.


 LAUNCH PUBLISHED EXPANSION
 Give an emerging market a practical electric starting point while WETA learns demand and operations.


 OPEN OVERLOOKED LINKS
 Test a waterfront-to-waterfront trip where road travel is indirect and large-ferry demand is not yet proven.


 START SMALL
 Begin with one bounded public problem and a clear operating hypothesis.


 GROW WITH DATA
 Add frequency, vessels or capacity only after real Bay performance is known.


 WETA DECIDES
 WETA retains network, safety, procurement and public-service authority.

DELIVERY GATES

Six questions to answer before passenger service
SAFETY + CERTIFICATION · Is the USCG pathway and operating envelope clear?
ACCESSIBLE PUBLIC SERVICE · Can boarding, seating, bikes, luggage and fare integration work?
TERMINAL + CHARGING · Are berth access, shore power and turnaround practical?
BAY OPERATING FIT · Do weather, sea state, range reserve, wake and navigation meet requirements?
CREW + MAINTENANCE · Can WETA train, staff, maintain and dispatch the service reliably?
ECONOMICS + PUBLIC VALUE · Does lifecycle cost improve useful access and rider outcomes?
