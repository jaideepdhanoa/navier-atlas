# TIGHTEN-AUDIT — Series B Deck (51-page r20 build)
*End-to-end read, every page, Aug 5 2026. Deck audited: `Navier Investor Deck — Series B (2).pdf`. No edits made.*

## 1. Narrative read-through

The new opening arc (1→5) **lands**: claim → vision → ladder → graveyard is exactly the SpaceX-clean spine, and the ladder's "every rung is a business that funds the next" sets up everything after. The acts also land in ladder order: people (18–25) → cargo (26–29) → defense (30) → money (31–34). Momentum sags in three places: **(a) pages 7–8 + 11–13** — four slides of problem/lever/first-principles/platform that say overlapping things before the product proof arrives; the reader is 13 pages in before feeling traction. **(b) Page 28 ("Each Step Funds the Next")** re-argues slide 4's ladder inside the cargo chapter — the story stalls to repeat itself. **(c) Page 33 ("Why We Win")** is a text recap between the emotional close (32) and the ask (34); it deflates the full-circle moment. The appendix is where the deck bloats: 40, 41, 47, 48 are legacy light-template slides that repeat main-flow content in an older voice and visibly pre-date the rebuild. Cut the repeats and the deck reads as one continuous 30-slide argument instead of a 34-slide argument with echoes.

## 3. Duplicates & contradictions

**Duplicate / overlapping slides**
- **7 ↔ 8 ↔ 12 ↔ 41**: "three costs / three levers / first principles / four problems" — four framings of the same argument. Keep one merged 7+8; cut 12 and 41.
- **28 ↔ 4 ↔ 18**: "each step funds the next" is slide 4's thesis and slide 18's structure; 28 is the third telling. Cut 28.
- **40 ↔ 7/8**: appendix "Why Now" repeats the main-flow why-now in the legacy light template. Cut 40.
- **47 ↔ 25**: two pipeline slides; 25 (sourced, quantified) supersedes 47. Cut 47.
- **33 ↔ 17/38**: "Why We Win" recaps the competitive slides. Cut 33.
- **45 ↔ 46**: both are Navy-validation testimonials. Merge.
- **Suspected 16-vs-17 duplication: NOT a duplication.** 16 = what Quanta unlocks (markets/map); 17 = defense-comp table. Both earn their place. Likewise appendix 38 (commercial comps) is complementary to 17 (defense comps), not a duplicate.
- **15 + 16** are consecutive Quanta slides but function as hero + substance; keep both (merge only if the count must drop below 29).

**Contradictions / factual flags (page + figure)**
1. **p24 "$1.1T+ top-down ceiling" vs p39 "The Opportunity $600B+"** — the legacy appendix slide was re-sourced downward but the main-flow market slide still carries the old $1.1T. Align p24 to the sourced $600B+ (or re-source $1.1T; fail-closed says use $600B+).
2. **p15 title "Introducing the N30 Quanta"** — violates the N30 ≠ Quanta rule. Body stats are fine (~2,000 NMi at 20 kts ✓).
3. **p44 "N30 — codename Quanta" (×2)** — same naming violation, plus "N120 — codename Morpheus" internal jargon.
4. **p21 vs p36 maintenance**: p21 (Maldives) shows N30 "Maintenance & servicing $25.0K" and energy $12.3K; p36 (premium markets) shows $40K maintenance sub + $25K parts, energy $15K. Different markets/duty can explain it, but the two schedules look identical (8 legs, ~300 days) — add a reconciling footnote or align.
5. **p10 / p13 ordering**: "GMVP" used on p10 before it's defined on p13.
6. **p46 footer "NAVIER OVERVIEW DECK"** — legacy deck footer left in a Series B deck.
7. **p25 "contracted demand per dollar raised" comparatives** (Arc ~$1, Saronic ~$0.15) — no visible source; verify or soften (fail-closed).
8. **p22 "$262K/yr recurring per vessel"** — confirm it traces to the partner model.
9. **Old-figure sweep: CLEAN.** No live page shows $15K/$10K maintenance-parts, $900K, 13 months, $780K, $650K, $120K lease, 12% yield, or 2,400 NMi. $70M appears only on parked p51. (p36's $15K/$10K are energy/software line items; p21's $120K is the fleet software tier; p48's 2,400 is tons.)
