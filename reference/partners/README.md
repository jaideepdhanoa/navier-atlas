# Public partner research

A reusable, source-linked reference library for partner research and presentation preparation. This is an explicit public-only subset—not the private relationship or deal knowledge base.

## Add or update a partner

1. Identify the correct company and business unit; recover existing research before repeating it.
2. Save factual summaries with stable source IDs, primary URLs, source/access dates, evidence classes and limitations. Separate reported capabilities, plans and historical evidence from current readiness.
3. Store `README.md`, `public-facts.md`, `facts.json`, `sources.json` / `.csv`, and a sanitation check under the partner's directory. Count unique primary URLs separately from source IDs and negative-search notes.
4. Exclude private emails/calls, personal contacts, confidential company figures, deal terms and proposed joint work. Keep those at their approved restricted access level.
5. Recheck time-sensitive facts before external use. A clean structural check is not independent factual verification or partner agreement.

For standalone partner PDFs, use the [two-pager toolkit](../../deck-studio/two-pagers/partner-two-pager/README.md) and its [Skill](../../deck-studio/two-pagers/partner-two-pager/SKILL.md). Full slide decks remain in [Deck Studio](../../deck-studio/README.md).
