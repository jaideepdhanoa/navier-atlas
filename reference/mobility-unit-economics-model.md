## Four archetype scenarios + a charter model

All scenarios benchmark **N30 vs a diesel comparable**, 20 nm reference trip (Nantucket↔Martha's Vineyard, Miami↔Key Biscayne, San Diego↔Coronado).

### 1 · Luxury Mobility (base)
- 6 pax cap (kept at 6 to avoid US Subchapter-T ferry trigger), 60% load → 3.6 pax/trip
- 85% monthly op capacity → 306 op days/yr; 5 trips/day → **1,530 trips/yr**
- Price **$125/pax** → **$450/trip** → **$688.5K/yr revenue**
- Comps: helicopter $200/pax, small plane $100/pax
- N30 capex **$900K** vs Hinckley Picnic Boat $1.2M
- Energy $0.063/kWh, 114 kWh battery, $7.18/charge, 2 trips/charge → annual energy **$5.5K** (vs diesel $97.9K)
- Maintenance $10K (vs $80K diesel); captain $120K; overhead $20K → **variable cost $155.5K** (vs diesel $317.9K)

### 2 · Aggressive Luxury Mobility
- 75% load → 4.5 pax/trip; 90% op cap → 324 days; 7 trips/day → **2,268 trips/yr**
- Price **$200/pax** → **$900/trip** → **$2.04M/yr revenue**
- Same $900K capex; energy at $0.27/kWh → $34.9K/yr; variable cost **$184.9K** (vs diesel $365.2K)

### 3 · Mass-Market Mobility
- 8 pax cap, 60% load → 4.8 pax/trip; 70% op cap → 252 days; 10 trips/day → **2,520 trips/yr**
- Price **$65/pax** (premium over $45 ferry, justified by 40min vs 60min + comfort/frequency) → **$312/trip** → **$786K/yr revenue**
- N30 (future product) capex **$300K** = World Cat 325 DC comp; variable cost **$198.8K** (vs diesel $351.3K)
- General: energy $0.32/mile; total cost ~$3.50/mile; SF Ferry comp $141/mile @ 315 pax

### 4 · Island-Hopping Boat Charter
- 4-hr trips, 2/day, 40 nm; 60% op cap → 216 days → **432 trips/yr**
- Price **$1,500/hr** (vs Hinckley $1,243) → **$5.18M/yr revenue** (vs diesel $4.30M); variable cost $163.3K (vs $263.8K)

## Headline benchmarks to reuse
- **N30 capex:** $900K (luxury config) / $300K (future mass-market product)
- **Energy:** ~$5.5K–$35K/yr vs diesel $98K–$161K/yr — the green-opex wedge
- **Maintenance:** $10K vs $50–80K diesel
- **Captain:** $120K/yr (largest opex line — the autonomy-ready upside)
- **Price points:** mass $65/pax · luxury $125/pax · aggressive $200/pax · charter $1,500/hr
- **Comps:** helicopter $200/pax · small plane $100/pax · ferry $45/pax · Hinckley $1.2M · World Cat $300K
- **Util assumptions:** 60–90% op capacity; tropical winter repositioning assumed to keep year-round utilization
