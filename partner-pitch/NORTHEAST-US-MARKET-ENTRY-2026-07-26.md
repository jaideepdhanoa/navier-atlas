# Navier — Northeast US Market Entry: Partners & Largest Opportunities
*2026-07-26 · four Atlas markets: New York Harbor · Cape Cod & Islands · Boston & New England · The Hamptons & East End*

## Opportunities, ranked

| # | Market | Posture | Size / proof | The wedge |
|---|--------|:---:|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | **New York Harbor** | P0 | Largest urban water market in the Americas — SI Ferry **16.7M** (2024), NYC Ferry record **7.4M** (2024), our Atlas sizes **~32.3M** water trips/yr | Congestion pricing (live Jan 2025) pushing trips to water + the **unclaimed airport-water link** (LGA Marine Air Terminal, JFK) |
| 2 | **Cape Cod & Islands** | P1 | One of the busiest US seasonal ferry systems — **~2.9M** pax, summer capacity limits | Steamship Authority is **grid-blocked** on conventional electric ferries; foiling's **~80% lower energy/trip** is the answer |
| 3 | **Boston & New England** | P1 | Dense harbour commuter spine (MBTA + private: Long Wharf/Rowes Wharf → Hingham, Hull, Harbor Islands) | State **net-zero-by-2050** mandate + **offshore-wind** crew-transfer (dual-use) |
| 4 | **The Hamptons & East End** | P2 | Highest-wealth US leisure market; lower volume, highest yield | **Greenfield public corridor** — Suffolk County/NYMTC study (approved Feb 2026, to ~2028); **near-zero wake** answers the harbormaster's shoreline concern |

## Partner target map

**Incumbent ferry operators** (fleet-upgrade / JV / vessel supply)
- **Hornblower Group** — highest-leverage single target; operates *both* NYC Ferry and Boston Harbor City Cruises (spans markets #1 and #3).
- **Seastreak** (NYC/NJ + seasonal Manhattan→Nantucket/MV/Hamptons), **Hy-Line Cruises** (Hyannis→Nantucket/MV), **Cross Sound Ferry / Viking Fleet / North & South Ferry** (East End, Shelter Island, Block Island).

**Public authorities & agencies** (operator / regulator)
- **NYC EDC** (owns the NYC Ferry program), **NYC DOT** (Staten Island Ferry), **Port Authority of NY & NJ** + **Massport** (airport-water links: LGA / JFK / Logan), **MBTA** (Boston Harbor ferries), **Massachusetts Steamship Authority** (Cape & Islands lifeline), **Suffolk County + NY Metropolitan Transportation Council** (East End study sponsors). *NYC Ferry, MBTA Ferry and Lyft already exist as tracked partners in our repo.*

**Super-app / ride-hail** (demand + booking layer)
- **Uber** and **Lyft** — both flagship in NYC; Lyft is already framed in our Atlas as "the multimodal platform, on the water." Same "water tier inside the app" model as Grab / Careem / DiDi.

**Premium / aviation-adjacent**
- **Blade** — Manhattan↔Hamptons seaplanes into Sag Harbor, Shelter Island, Montauk (East River Aqua Lounge) + Manhattan↔JFK. A premium distribution partner for the water legs, or the incumbent to undercut; its termini map onto our East End routes.

**Offshore wind (dual-use)**
- **Vineyard Wind / Avangrid, Ørsted** and their crew-transfer operators out of New Bedford / Boston.

**Hospitality (Navier Experience)**
- Gurney's (Montauk), and high-end Nantucket / Newport / Shelter Island properties for premium point-to-point.

## Recommended sequence
1. **Lead with New York Harbor** — Hornblower/NYC EDC on the operator side, Uber or Lyft on the demand side, and a **Port Authority airport-link pilot** as the wedge (LGA Marine Air Terminal is the cleanest first route).
2. **Cape & Islands as the decarbonization pitch** — take the "you can't electrify conventionally, but you can foil" case straight to the Steamship Authority (+ Hy-Line for the premium tier).
3. **Plant a flag in the Hamptons now** — get Navier into the Suffolk County/NYMTC study while it's scoping, leading with near-zero wake.
4. **Boston as the anchor + dual-use base** — MBTA harbour tier plus offshore-wind CTV.
