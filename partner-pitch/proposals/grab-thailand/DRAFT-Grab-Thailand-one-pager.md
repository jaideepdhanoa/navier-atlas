## The one line
Navier builds **autonomous-ready, all-electric hydrofoiling boats** — the "driverless" surface-water layer for Thailand's two coasts and its river megacity. Demand already lives in the Grab app; the water tier is unclaimed.

## Why Thailand, why now
Thailand is one of the strongest fits we've mapped anywhere. Three clusters, one country story — **the water layer for Thailand's two coasts and its river megacity**:

| Cluster | The wedge | Signature water routes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gulf / Koh Samui** | A million-plus air arrivals feed a constant island-hop flow today served only by slow, weather-fragile diesel ferries. | Samui ↔ Koh Phangan (10nm) · Samui ↔ Koh Tao (35nm) · Donsak mainland crossings · Four Seasons ↔ Ang Thong Marine Park |
| **Andaman / Phuket** | Resort + island-hop premium where every leg is a long road drive or a rough boat. | Phuket ↔ Phang Nga / James Bond (20nm) · Phuket ↔ Phi Phi (25nm) · Phuket ↔ Krabi (40nm) · Similan (55nm) |
| **Bangkok** | A river-commuter megacity with chronic road gridlock and a Gulf of islands within hybrid reach. | Chao Phraya premium river tier · Bangkok → Pattaya / Koh Chang / Samui gulf gateway |

## The frame for Grab (three lenses)
1. **Autonomy / future mobility** — Navier's control systems and autonomy roadmap are AV parallels, applied to water. Software-defined, autonomy-ready fleet that gets cheaper to run over time.
2. **Super-app water tier** — in Thailand demand already lives in-app; the premium water layer is the one mode no platform serves. A branded, in-app foiling tier.
3. **Congestion + island connectivity** — congestion-beating river hops in Bangkok; reliable island transfers in the Gulf and Andaman that hold schedule through monsoon chop.

## Why it's real, not a concept
- **Hydrofoiling step-change:** the hull flies clear of the water — ~10× more efficient than a planing boat, 30+ kt cruise, smooth and near-silent.
- **No new infrastructure:** rides on berths and jetties that already exist. No vertiports, no rights-of-way. Pioneer II needs only a berth pedestal; the long-range Quanta-LR hybrid needs no charging infrastructure at all.
- **Range-honest:** short island/river legs fly all-electric (Pioneer II, ≤70nm). Long gulf runs (e.g. Bangkok↔Samui ~205nm) use the Quanta-LR hybrid — flagged as roadmap, never overclaimed.
- **Proof point:** a ~100-vessel electric-foiling network is being built in the Maldives ($100M; first vessels 2026) — the directly transferable inter-island playbook.

## What a low-friction pilot looks like
Start in **one cluster** — the Samui–Phangan–Tao triangle is the cleanest first pilot: dense, proven flows, painful weather-fragile ferries, premium guests, jetties already in place. Prove the in-app premium hop, then extend to Phuket/Andaman and the Bangkok river.
