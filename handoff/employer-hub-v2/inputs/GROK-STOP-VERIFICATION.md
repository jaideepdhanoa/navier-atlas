# Grok stop verification

Audited against the rule that a landing must be a presently usable dock/berth/float for a 30–45 ft commercial passenger vessel (not merely a ramp or planned facility).

| Stop | Verdict (PASS / PASS-with-flag / FAIL) | Actual facility name | Evidence (1-line) | Source URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coyote Point Marina (San Mateo) | PASS | Coyote Point Marina | San Mateo County says visitors are always welcome, boats up to 45 ft can be accommodated, and slips/side ties run 24–60+ ft. | https://www.smcgov.org/parks/coyote-point-marina |
| Port of Redwood City Municipal Marina (451 Seaport Ct) | PASS | Port of Redwood City's Municipal Marina | Port says it operates a 190-berth marina for vessels up to 50 ft with 10 ft low-tide depth and accepts temporary berth applications. | https://www.redwoodcityport.com/marina |
| Alviso Marina (San Jose) | FAIL | Alviso Marina County Park boat-launch area | Santa Clara County describes concrete launch ramps and two floating docks for launching/access, but no marina/guest berthing; this is a launch/paddling site, not a passenger-berth landing. | https://parks.santaclaracounty.gov/locations/alviso-marina-county-park |
| Hayward Landing | FAIL | Eden Landing Ecological Reserve launch (Mt Eden Creek) | Water Trail identifies the high-freeboard dock/low float specifically for non-motorized small boats and warns Bay-mouth mudflats can become unnavigable; no 30–45-ft passenger landing. | https://sfbaywatertrail.org/trailhead/eden-landing/ |
| San Leandro Marina | FAIL | Former San Leandro Marina | City says the San Leandro Marina was permanently closed in January 2023; it is not a presently usable harbor landing. | https://www.sanleandro.org/1359/Shoreline-Facilities-Updates |
| Pittsburg Marina | PASS | City of Pittsburg Marina | City marina lists 575 berths, 50-ft slips, 50–75-ft side ties, 7.5-ft average depth, and guest berths. | https://www.pittsburgmarina.com/amenities-services |
| Benicia Marina | PASS | Benicia Marina | Operator explicitly offers long- and short-term (guest) berthing and 24/7 pumpout at the active 266 East B Street marina. | https://beniciamarina.net/ |
| Hercules Waterfront | FAIL | No built ferry landing; proposed Hercules Hub ferry terminal | Hercules’ official project page describes the Hub as a development/new train stop; the ferry terminal remains proposed/planned rather than an operating facility. | https://www.herculesca.gov/government/planning/hercules-projects/intermodal-transit-center |
| Emery Cove Marina (Emeryville) | PASS | Emery Cove Yacht Harbor | Operator explicitly offers short-term guest docking and has 430 berths ranging from 30–60 ft. | https://emerycove.com/ |
| Berkeley Marina guest docks K-102/K-104 | PASS | Berkeley Marina K-Dock guest slips K-102 and K-104 | City of Berkeley directs guest boaters to pull into K-102 or K-104; overnight visitors are accepted, including 30–49-ft vessels. | https://berkeleyca.gov/community-recreation/waterfront/guest-boaters |
| Norwalk CT | PASS | Norwalk Seaport Dock, 90 Water Street (Sheffield Island ferry dock) | Norwalk Seaport Association runs scheduled, ticketed USCG passenger ferry service and states cruises depart from its dock at 90 Water Street. | https://www.seaport.org/ferry-schedule |
| Port Washington NY Town Dock | PASS-with-flag | North Hempstead Town Dock, Port Washington | Town identifies active dockage for vessels 89 ft and under (insurance and certificate of inspection required) and says its moorings include water-taxi service; commercial boarding still needs town/operator approval. | https://www.northhempsteadny.gov/departments/parks_recreation/towndock.php |
| Throgs Neck (Bronx) | PASS-with-flag | NYC Ferry Throgs Neck (Ferry Point Park) landing | NYC Ferry’s official route lists a real landing at Ferry Point Park, Bronx 10465, with scheduled service; label must not imply a separate landing elsewhere in Throgs Neck. | https://www.ferry.nyc/routes-and-schedules/rockaway-soundview/ |

## Recommended actions

- **Keep:** Coyote Point Marina; Port of Redwood City Municipal Marina; Pittsburg Marina; Benicia Marina; Emery Cove Yacht Harbor; Berkeley Marina K-102/K-104; Norwalk Seaport Dock (90 Water Street).
- **Rename-to-correct-landing:** Throgs Neck → **NYC Ferry Throgs Neck (Ferry Point Park)**. Port Washington → **North Hempstead Town Dock, Port Washington** and retain the commercial-approval flag.
- **Cull:** Alviso Marina; Hayward Landing; San Leandro Marina; Hercules Waterfront.
