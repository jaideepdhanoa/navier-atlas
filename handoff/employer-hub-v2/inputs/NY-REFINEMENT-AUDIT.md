# NY refinement audit (corrected landing standard)

Audited 2026-08-14 under the corrected standard: a usable landing = any pier, marina guest dock, floating dock, or public berth where a 30–45 ft passenger vessel (~3–4 ft draft) can tie up and load passengers, with adequate low-tide depth and a navigable channel. Terminal buildings NOT required. Still FAIL: silted/non-navigable, docks removed/closed, kayak-only, ramp-only. Sweep target: marina-grade nodes NOT in the current 32-stop NY network that add real residential commuter catchments feeding Manhattan / Brooklyn / Stamford employment.

Population figures are approximate (US Census orders of magnitude) and marked "~".

## Recommended additions (ranked)

1. **Yonkers Recreation Pier** — public downtown pier with docking, Metro-North across the street, 211k-person city, proven prior ferry landing; the single biggest unserved catchment on the list.
2. **New Rochelle Municipal Marina (Echo Bay)** — 350-slip, 10-ft city marina selling daily dockage, under a booming 80k-person downtown feeding both Manhattan and Stamford.
3. **Atlantic Highlands Municipal Harbor** — municipal harbor that already berths commercial head boats in the region's proven ferry-commuter basin; run as feeder/complement to incumbent Seastreak.
4. **Bridgeport Harbor Marina (Steelpointe)** — new 200+ slip deep-water marina in Connecticut's largest city (~148k).
5. **Milford Lisman Landing** — all-transient city marina, 7 ft MLW, walkable downtown near Metro-North; ready-made float.
6. **Liberty Landing Marina (Jersey City)** — 520 deep-water slips (22/12 ft); new Morris Canal Basin catchment plus the network's best ops/charging base.
7. **Newport Marina (Jersey City)** — 400 ft of floating dock in the densest unserved Gold Coast residential pocket between Paulus Hook and Hoboken.
8. **Moonbeam Great Kills Marina (Staten Island)** — 15 ft floating-dock marina serving the south-shore commuter desert with a direct lower-bay run to Wall St.
9. **Marine Basin Marina (Bensonhurst)** — commercial berthing on offer in southern Brooklyn's transit desert; confirm dredged depth on site visit before scheduling.

**Watchlist (MAYBE):** ONE°15 Brooklyn Marina (use as verified landing/backup for existing BBP Pier 6 stop); Huntington Harbor (docks verified, distance economics unproven); Oyster Bay Marine Center (great dock, small leisure catchment); Mamaroneck Harbor Island (needs village allocation); Rye Boat Basin (6 ft approach, 37 ft cap); Northport Village Dock (leisure tier); Westport Ned Dimes (tight town allocation); Minneford Marina, City Island (leisure/waypoint).

**Confirmed OUT:** North Cove Marina (closed for redevelopment; catchment already served by stop 3); West 79th St Boat Basin and Dyckman Marina (closed for reconstruction to ~2028 — revisit); World's Fair Marina (docks closed indefinitely); Sheepshead Bay piers (passenger pick-up/drop-off banned by NYC Parks since May 2019 — advocacy target only); Cold Spring Harbor (4 ft dockside, 31 ft max); Theodore Roosevelt Memorial Park, Oyster Bay (ramp-only); New Haven Long Wharf (UNVERIFIED — no confirmed passenger berth; watch 2026–27 rebuild).

**Existing-stop flag:** Lincoln Harbor Yacht Club (network stop 8) is under construction — office building demolished, facilities relocated across the street; confirm berth assignment with the marina before service start.
