# Bay Area Employer Water Network — Node Inventory (v2)

Research basis: official operator, city/port, marina, and authority pages linked in each row. **Active** means a currently documented scheduled passenger service; a marina/dock is included only where the source documents a real berth or guest tie-up. “Unknown” is used where the source does not establish a field.

## System context

- **San Francisco Bay Ferry / WETA:** The Water Emergency Transportation Authority (WETA) operates the regional SF Bay Ferry network. Its current terminal/service context includes Downtown San Francisco, Oakland/Jack London Square, Alameda Main Street, Alameda Seaplane Lagoon, Alameda Harbor Bay, Richmond, South San Francisco/Oyster Point, Vallejo, Redwood City (service status below), and event/limited-service landings such as Oracle Park and Mare Island. See [routes and schedules](https://sanfranciscobayferry.com/routes-schedules/) and [WETA future projects](https://sanfranciscobayferry.com/our-ferry-future/).
- **Golden Gate Ferry:** Golden Gate Bridge, Highway and Transportation District operates scheduled ferry service at Larkspur, Sausalito, Tiburon, and San Francisco Ferry Terminal. See [terminal locations](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/riding-the-ferry/terminal-locations-parking/) and [riding the ferry](https://www.goldengate.org/ferry/riding-the-ferry/).
- **Other scheduled operators:** Blue & Gold Fleet operates passenger service from Pier 41 and the Angel Island/Tiburon network; Treasure Island’s separate private ferry is operated by Prop SF through Treasure Island Development Corporation (TIDC), not WETA.

## No landing exists

These are demand areas for which no eligible **current passenger landing** was documented. A proposed, closed, boat-launch-only, or unverified shoreline site is not promoted to a node.

- **Mission Bay/China Basin ferry landing:** project is still in design/permitting and construction phases; Port schedule lists Phase 2b ferry landing construction in 2026/27. No current scheduled landing.
- **Pier 70 / Crane Cove Park:** no current ferry terminal or qualifying scheduled passenger landing was verified in the Port materials reviewed. Crane Cove is waterfront public space, not automatically a ferry node.
- **Redwood City scheduled ferry terminal:** the Port has a real municipal marina, but no current scheduled ferry service was verified; the marina is listed above as a non-scheduled dock, not as a ferry terminal.
- **Foster City:** no eligible scheduled ferry terminal or verified guest/commercial passenger dock documented.
- **Burlingame:** no eligible scheduled ferry terminal or verified guest/commercial passenger dock documented.
- **Palo Alto / East Palo Alto shoreline:** no eligible ferry terminal, public dock with documented guest/commercial berthing, or scheduled passenger landing documented.
- **Berkeley Municipal Pier:** ferry access is a City/WETA project in **Design** and environmental/permitting work; the existing pier was closed for structural problems. Berkeley Marina guest docks exist but are not the proposed ferry terminal and have no scheduled service.
- **Fremont / Newark:** no eligible ferry terminal or verified guest/commercial passenger dock documented.
- **San Rafael:** no current scheduled passenger terminal or qualifying public/guest/commercial landing was verified in the sources reviewed. Larkspur is the documented Golden Gate Ferry terminal serving the central Marin catchment.
- **Antioch:** the City marina page confirms a marina/fuel facility, but the material reviewed did not establish guest or commercial passenger berthing; no eligible network landing is promoted. No scheduled passenger ferry was verified ([City marina](https://www.antiochca.gov/359/Marina)).
- **Martinez:** no current scheduled passenger ferry terminal or qualifying landing was verified in the sources reviewed.
- **Benicia:** no current scheduled passenger ferry terminal or qualifying landing was verified in the sources reviewed; Vallejo is the documented WETA terminal for the Solano/Napa catchment.
