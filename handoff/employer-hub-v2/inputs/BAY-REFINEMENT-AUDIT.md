# Bay refinement audit (corrected landing standard)

Audited 2026-08-14 under the corrected standard: a usable landing = any pier, marina guest dock, floating dock, or public berth where a 30–45 ft passenger vessel (~3–4 ft draft) can tie up and load passengers, with adequate low-tide depth and a navigable channel to open water. Terminal buildings NOT required. Still FAIL: silted/non-navigable water, removed/closed docks, kayak-only launches, ramp-only sites.
