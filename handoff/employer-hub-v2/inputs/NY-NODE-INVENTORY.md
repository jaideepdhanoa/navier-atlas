## No landing exists

The following are demand areas or proposed links for which research did not verify a currently usable passenger landing suitable for network-node promotion. Existing ramps, seawalls, private yacht-club floats, or a marina elsewhere in the same municipality do not substitute for a public/scheduled node.

- **LaGuardia airport passenger-ferry node:** the Marine Air Terminal is a real historic waterfront terminal, but the airport identifies Terminal A as closed to the public and no scheduled passenger ferry was verified. World's Fair Marina is separately a real marina but is currently listed closed, with only limited transient dockage by Dockmaster contact.
- **Stapleton, Staten Island:** waterfront exists, but no current public scheduled passenger terminal was verified; use St. George instead.
- **Dyckman/Upper Manhattan scheduled ferry:** Dyckman Marina is a real dock and is inventoried above as a marina, but no scheduled passenger service was verified; a future ferry/eco-dock concept is not promoted as current.
- **Stamford and Norwalk commuter ferries:** Harbor Point, Norwalk Cove and Norwalk Visitors Docks are real marina/public-dock assets, but no active scheduled commuter ferry was verified. Do not treat past studies/proposals as service.
- **New Haven Long Wharf commuter ferry:** Long Wharf has real public waterfront/boathouse/port facilities, but no current scheduled passenger ferry landing was verified.
- **Glen Cove Manhattan ferry:** the built terminal is inventoried as a former/planned node; no active operator or launched commuter service was verified.
- **Long Beach passenger ferry:** the cited city facility is a boat-launch ramp, not a verified passenger landing; no scheduled passenger ferry service was found.
- **Sag Harbor–Greenport passenger ferry:** Long Wharf and Greenport docks are real, but the proposed direct passenger link was not verified as operating; North Ferry service is instead via Shelter Island.
- **Huntington Harbor, Northport, Port Washington, Oyster Bay, Freeport and Hampton Bays/Shinnecock:** real town/marina docks are inventoried above, but no scheduled passenger ferry to Manhattan or another regional employment center was verified.
