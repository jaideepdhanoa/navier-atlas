- Use **$1M/vessel economics**.
- Use **Cost · Convenience · Comfort** as the hospitality operator frame.
- **No SOM/SAM/TAM/GMV ladder** in hospitality decks.
- **Slide 2 stays KPI-free** and gets its own image/prompt; it must not reuse the Three C’s background.
- Use **plain English**. Do not leak internal finance/model words into slide titles, subtitles, captions, or labels.
- Unit-economics appendix uses the Minor deck’s table format: Revenue build · Annual run cost · Result per vessel/year.
- All routes are **property/demand anchored**. Bangkok is allowed as a hotel-curated city-to-river gateway, not as an open river transit product.
- Unknown route IDs, dock rights, or exact distances stay **null / pending** until sealed.

## 2. Centara narrative thesis

### One-line thesis

**Centara already sits on Thailand’s best water-facing guest journeys. Navier turns those stays into a cleaner, quieter, more premium way to arrive, move, and explore — starting with six Thai clusters where the hotel footprint and destination demand overlap.**

### Longer narrative

Centara is not a single resort opportunity. In Thailand, it is a domestic hospitality network that touches the country’s highest-value leisure arcs: Bangkok’s river gateway, Hua Hin’s heritage coast, Pattaya and Koh Chang on the Eastern Gulf, Phuket’s Andaman beaches, Krabi and Phi Phi’s island scenery, and Samui’s Gulf-island circuit.

The common guest pain is simple: Thailand’s coast sells ease and beauty, but many premium transfers still feel fragmented — road traffic, exposed pier handoffs, inconsistent day-trip operators, and arrivals that undersell the resort experience. Navier gives Centara a branded electric water layer that makes the journey feel like part of the stay.

The proposal should lead with experience and operator practicality: **Cost · Convenience · Comfort**. The economics appendix proves that the same vessels can also be commercially useful on short, repeatable, guest-paid routes.

## 3. Six Centara Thailand clusters

### Cluster 1 — Bangkok river gateway

**Role in deck:** urban feeder, brand gateway, MICE/leisure demand source.  
**Important framing:** Bangkok is not a coastal resort cluster. Include it because Jaideep explicitly wants Bangkok as the sixth cluster and because Centara’s Bangkok flagship can introduce guests to a premium river journey before/after coastal stays.

**Hero assets**
- Centara Grand & Bangkok Convention Centre at CentralWorld — A-fit demand anchor.
- Centara Grand at Central Plaza Ladprao Bangkok — secondary MICE/leisure feeder.
- Centara Life Hotel Bangkok Phra Nakhon — old-city river-adjacent feeder.

**Narrative hook**
Bangkok is where the Centara relationship starts: convention guests, retail travelers, family leisure, and outbound resort guests. Navier gives them a polished Chao Phraya arrival layer without pretending Centara owns the river.

**Candidate corridor**
- CentralWorld guest transfer → Sathorn / ICONSIAM / old-city river circuit.  
  Status: working concept; property is the demand endpoint, water leg uses public/private pier access still to be sealed.

### Cluster 2 — Western Gulf: Hua Hin / Cha-Am

**Role in deck:** heritage weekend coast and Bangkok leisure extension.

**Hero assets**
- Centara Grand Beach Resort Hua Hin — A-fit; historic beachfront flagship.
- Centara Life Sea Breeze Resort Hua Hin — B-fit coastal support.
- Centara Life Cha Am Beach Resort Hua Hin — B-fit coastal support.

**Narrative hook**
Hua Hin is the classic Bangkok weekend escape. The Centara Grand Hua Hin story is especially powerful because the resort is heritage, beachfront, and premium: Navier can make the arrival and local excursions feel as considered as the property itself.

**Candidate corridor**
- Centara Grand Hua Hin → Khao Takiab / Cha-Am coastal hop.  
  Status: working; requires dock/beach operations validation.

### Cluster 3 — Eastern Gulf: Pattaya / Jomtien / Sriracha / Koh Chang

**Role in deck:** highest-density mainland resort cluster plus island extension.

**Hero assets**
- Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya — A-fit beachfront family flagship.
- Centara Koh Chang Tropicana Resort — A-fit island resort.
- COSI Pattaya Wong Amat Beach, Centara Life Maris Resort Jomtien, Centara Sonrisa Sriracha — B-fit cluster support.

**Narrative hook**
The Eastern Gulf gives Centara both scale and variety: Pattaya’s family resort demand, Jomtien/Wong Amat density, Sriracha’s Eastern Seaboard base, and Koh Chang’s island-stay logic. It is the best cluster for showing that Navier can support both short excursions and true island arrivals.

**Candidate corridors**
- Centara Grand Mirage Pattaya → Koh Larn.
- Trat / Laem Ngop ferry gateway → Centara Koh Chang Tropicana.

## 6. Destination fact base for deck and optional partner page

### Thailand national context
- Thailand had roughly **35 million international tourist arrivals in 2024** and tourism revenue around **THB 1.8 trillion** in official TAT reporting.
- Thailand’s 2025 tourism plan targeted **36–39 million international visitors** and roughly **THB 1.98–2.23 trillion** in tourism revenue.
- The point for Centara: this is not a niche resort experiment; Thailand is a scaled leisure platform where premium guest movement is visible and repeatable.

### Bangkok
- Chao Phraya river tourism already has scheduled hop-on/hop-off boat behavior, frequent departures, and named tourist piers.
- The point for Centara: Bangkok gives Centara a visible river gateway and MICE/leisure demand source, even though it is not a coastal resort cluster.

### Hua Hin / Cha-Am
- Hua Hin is a classic beach destination close to Bangkok, with heritage hotels, golf, wellness, family leisure, and weekend demand.
- The point for Centara: Centara Grand Hua Hin lets Navier tell a heritage-arrival story that feels premium but practical.

### Eastern Gulf: Pattaya / Koh Chang
- Chon Buri/Pattaya is one of Bangkok’s nearest beach markets and one of Thailand’s best-known resort corridors.
- Koh Chang is a large island destination in Trat with ferry-gateway dependence and resort-stay logic.
- The point for Centara: this cluster combines frequency and island pain — the two things a branded water layer needs.

### Phuket
- Phuket is Thailand’s best-known international resort island and an obvious premium leisure market.
- The point for Centara: Centara has enough Karon/Kata density for a property-graph story, not just a single-property route.

### Krabi / Phi Phi
- Krabi is defined by beaches, limestone cliffs, islands, and water access; Phi Phi strengthens the island-arrival story.
- The point for Centara: the water journey can become part of the destination, especially around Pai Plong Bay, Ao Nang, and Phi Phi.

### Samui / Gulf islands
- Samui has airport adjacency, beach resort demand, and natural inter-island logic with Koh Phangan.
- The point for Centara: Centara Reserve Samui creates a premium flagship, while Phangan gives the scaled-island extension.
