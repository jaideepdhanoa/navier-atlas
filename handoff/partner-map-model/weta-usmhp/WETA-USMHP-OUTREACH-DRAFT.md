# WETA × Navier partnership outreach — draft for Sampriti review

**Status:** Human-review draft only. Not sent.

**Suggested recipients:** WETA leadership / board contacts known directly to Sampriti

**Subject:** WETA × Navier — building the next layer of Bay water transit

Hi [Name],

Navier is a Bay Area company, and the Bay is deeply important to us. We would value the opportunity to explore a long-term partnership with WETA to expand what water transit can do for the region.

Our starting point is WETA’s public mission and operating reality—not a grant program. We believe a small, electric hydrofoil service layer could be evaluated alongside WETA to:

- add frequency or resilience on selected existing corridors;
- assess whether smaller vessels could augment—or, where the evidence supports it over time, replace selected trips more effectively;
- help open new corridors that conventional ferries cannot serve efficiently; and
- move packages alongside passengers where a real customer need and safe operating case can be established.

We have prepared a short partnership proposal for discussion:

[WETA × Navier — Bay Partnership Proposal](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1frwn6G6NrGdzxbJEqlO_EZ6M-vWF2dLYN8PWhGwHRpw/edit)

We also see the FY26 Marine Highway opportunity as a potential accelerator—not the premise of the relationship. We have not assumed a package customer, grant eligibility, route approval, procurement path, or WETA commitment. With WETA’s guidance, we propose first defining a useful passenger service and then separately testing whether a real package flow and compliant federal funding pathway exist.

Would you be open to a 45-minute working session to identify the public problem worth solving, the corridor types WETA would want screened, and the evidence required before considering a pilot?

Warmly,

Sampriti
