## Goa correction

Goa should not remain at a thin baseline. It should be promoted to a **full / marquee India tourism market** for Uber India, Rapido, Ola, Reliance, and Adani where relevant.

### Why current Goa is thin

The current Goa rows are mostly short ferry / marina hops, plus a Goa ↔ Mumbai long leg. They do not yet express the core tourism use cases:

1. airport / arrival transfers,
2. North Goa beach-resort circulation,
3. South Goa resort circulation,
4. Panaji / Mandovi / Old Goa visitor mobility,
5. premium hotel / marina / waterfront transfers,
6. event / nightlife congestion bypass,
7. Mumbai ↔ Goa Quanta-LR roadmap narrative.

### Goa expansion targets for this pass

Do not invent final route IDs. Treat these as Grok / Atlas registry expansion targets to bind against existing or newly approved boarding points.

| Cluster | Proposed corridor family | Proposal use case |

| Panaji / Mandovi | Panaji ↔ Dona Paula / Miramar / Old Goa / Divar-Chorao | city-waterfront mobility, heritage, visitor hops |

### Goa cleanup targets

The current route scan shows at least one likely false-positive / non-Goa artifact and a few poor endpoint labels. Before proposal export:

- remove or quarantine non-Goa false positives from Goa counts,
- normalize noisy endpoint labels,
- split commercial-now N30 routes from Quanta-LR roadmap,

2. **Kerala quality/depth check** — ensure Kochi/backwaters/airport-transfer routes are not too thin.
