## VERIFIED (primary-sourced, now referenced on deck S9/S19)

1. **MINT Fund (Maritime Innovation and Technology Fund)** — real. MPA co-funds **up to 50% of
   project costs** for maritime R&D/test-bedding by companies with a local presence; S$200M scale;
   rolling calls for proposals.
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/maritime-singapore/what-maritime-singapore-offers/programmes-to-support-your-maritime-business/mint-fund
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/maritime-innovation-technology-(mint)-fund-and-maritime-r-d

2. **TR 136 (2025)** — real. Technical Reference for electric harbour craft charging + battery-swap
   systems, published by MPA + EnterpriseSG via Singapore Standards Council, March 2025.
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/singapore-introduces-new-standard-for-electric-harbour-craft-(e-hc)-charging-infrastructure

3. **Marina South Pier e-HC charging point** — real. First public pilot charging point (350kW class)
   launched April 2024 at Marina South Pier — i.e., at the exact gateway pier in our candidate links.
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/first-pilot-trial-for-electric-harbour-craft-charging-point-launched-at-marina-south-pier

4. **Port-dues concessions** — real. Maritime Singapore Green Initiative / Green Port Programme
   grants port-dues concessions for clean vessels. Exact percentages vary by scheme/vessel class —
   deck cites the programme, not a number.
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/maritime-singapore/sustainability/maritime-singapore-green-initiative

5. **MPA e-HC reference-design EOI precedent** — real. July 2023 EOI to design/promote e-HC adoption;
   11 proposals selected (Feb 2024) for reference designs; separate EOI drew 12 financing + 7
   insurance proposals. Navier fits an established pattern.
   - https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/call-for-expression-of-interest-to-design-and-promote-adoption-of-electric-harbour-craft-in-singapore
   - https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/shipping/022324-singapores-mpa-picks-11-proposals-for-electric-harbor-craft-design

## CHECKED, DIFFERENT THAN CLAIMED

- **LTA** — mandate is land transport only (MRT/bus/active mobility, "car-lite"); **no water
  transport role** exists today. The search text's "LTA deems waterways impractical" is unsourced.
  Implication: water transit is a genuine white space between agencies; MPA is the regulator of
  everything on the water and stays the front door. LTA is a potential *second audience* for the
  zero-land-take commuter story, best introduced via/with MPA — not a parallel cold track.
