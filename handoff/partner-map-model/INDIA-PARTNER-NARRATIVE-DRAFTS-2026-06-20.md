## Shared India thesis

India is not a single marine-mobility market; it is a portfolio of coastal, riverine and island transfer problems layered over very large land-mobility platforms. Navier’s wedge is to connect those platforms to routes where the road network is slow, tourism demand is concentrated, or ferry demand already proves the waterway.

Priority markets for this slice:

1. Goa — marquee tourism expansion.
2. Mumbai / Navi Mumbai / Elephanta / Mandwa — marquee commuter + tourism corridor.
3. Kerala / Kochi / Vembanad — proven water-transit and island-city model.
4. Andaman & Nicobar — island mobility and tourism/harbour use case.
5. Gujarat coast — exact-bind expansion; Adani/Reliance overlay only.
6. Chennai / Tamil Nadu — exact-bind expansion with India-Sri Lanka ferry adjacency.
7. Vizag / Andhra — port-city and coastal tourism expansion lane.
8. Kolkata-Haldia / West Bengal — riverine commuter/logistics corridor.

Promotion rule: every newly promoted market needs at least **two local use cases**. Where exact bind is missing, keep as brief-only / economics-pending.

## Rapido India draft

### Positioning

Rapido is the best first India mobility partner for a **high-frequency feeder-to-water** story. Its public claim of 400+ cities and product spread across bikes, autos, cabs, parcel, travel and metro means the Navier pitch should not try to make Rapido a marine operator. It should make Rapido the demand and distribution layer around terminals, beach clusters, hotels and commuter nodes.

### Why now

India’s coastal and river cities already show the pain pattern Navier solves: long cross-bay road trips, constrained bridges, island communities tied to city jobs, and tourism flows concentrated around a few airports, ports and waterfronts. Rapido’s advantage is dense short-hop supply and low-friction booking. Navier’s advantage is turning the water crossing from an occasional ferry into a premium, scheduled, integrated mobility leg.

### Market narrative

- **Goa:** Rapido can own the short-hop feeder layer around Mopa/Dabolim, hotels, beaches and marinas while Navier handles the premium coastal leg. Goa’s 2025 tourism base of 10.8M arrivals gives the market enough demand for a marquee tourism phase.
- **Mumbai:** Rapido is a terminal feeder for Belapur, Nerul, DCT, JNPT, Gateway/Elephanta and Mandwa-style movements. The proposition is not replacing the water taxi; it is making the water option easier to access and book.
- **Kerala/Kochi:** Rapido can wrap first/last-mile distribution around Kochi Water Metro terminals and nearby island-city trips, where the public system already validates the travel behavior.
- **Andaman:** Keep as a lighter India derivative unless local availability is verified; the story is future inter-island tourism and harbour transfer support.
- **Expansion lanes:** Gujarat, Chennai/TN, Vizag and Kolkata-Haldia stay brief-only until exact city/route binds and local partner evidence are clean.

### Phase draft

- **Prove:** Goa + Mumbai + Kochi. Focus on app-distributed premium water legs connected to pickup/drop points, not broad city rollout.
- **Scale:** Add Andaman if local partner availability and tourism/airport anchors clear; deepen Mumbai and Goa into hotel/airport packages.
- **Mature:** Add Gujarat, Chennai/TN, Vizag and Kolkata-Haldia only where exact routes and two-use-case gates are satisfied.

### Guardrails

Use country-supported evidence for broad India presence, but do not claim exact Rapido city service in a market unless sourced locally.

## Ola India draft

### Positioning

Ola should be framed as a **national mobility and payments/distribution partner** for premium water legs, not as a pure rideshare clone. Ola’s public platform claims 250+ cities and 55 crore+ yearly rides, with products across bikes, autos, Prime sedans/SUVs, rentals and outstation. That makes Ola useful for airport, hotel, outstation and event-transfer packaging.

### Why now

The strongest India marine opportunities are not isolated ferry rides; they are bundled journeys: airport → hotel → waterfront → island/beach/port. Ola can connect those legs in a familiar booking environment while Navier supplies the differentiated water segment.

### Market narrative

- **Goa:** Package airport, resort and beach-cluster transfers as premium predictable journeys; tourism volume is the anchor.
- **Mumbai:** Pair land pickup with water transfer between Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Elephanta, JNPT, Mandwa and Karanja-style nodes.
- **Kerala/Kochi:** Use Ola as the city mobility layer around Water Metro proof points and premium backwater routes.
- **Andaman:** Treat as future tourism/island mobility unless Ola local service is verified.
- **Gujarat / Chennai / Vizag / Kolkata-Haldia:** Use as expansion prose only until exact Atlas and local service evidence exist.

### Phase draft

- **Prove:** airport/hotel packages in Goa; terminal transfers in Mumbai; Kochi terminal/backwater pilots.
- **Scale:** add bundled outstation/leisure flows and event/tourism packages.
- **Mature:** add river/port/coastal expansion lanes after route seal and demand anchors.

### Guardrails

Do not use Ola’s national scale to imply local operating status in Goa/Andaman/Gujarat coastal nodes without exact support.

## Uber India derivative draft

### Positioning

Uber India should be a **derivative proposal**, not a destructive replacement for the global Uber proposal. The derivative can use Uber’s official India city roster for exact city-supported evidence where rows match coastal/river markets.

### Why now

Uber’s official India city roster is unusually broad and includes many relevant coastal or river-adjacent localities. That gives Navier a safer exact-bind pathway than generic country claims, provided we extract only the rows we can defend.

### Market narrative

- **Goa:** Uber’s official roster includes Goa localities such as Canacona. Use exact rows for city-supported evidence, then focus the Navier story on tourism and premium resort/coastal transfers.
- **Mumbai/Maharashtra:** Uber rows include Mumbai-region and coastal Maharashtra candidates such as Alibag/Dahanu/Guhagar-style localities. Filter to Atlas-supported hierarchy before display.
- **Kerala:** Uber rows include multiple Kerala localities. Use exact extraction to support Kochi/Aluva/Cherthala-type terminal and backwater adjacency.
- **Chennai/Tamil Nadu:** Chennai is directly in the official roster; use it as the cleanest Tamil Nadu anchor.
- **Vizag/Andhra:** Extract Gajuwaka, Bheemunipatnam, Anakapalle and relevant Andhra coastal rows before any city-supported claim.
- **Kolkata-Haldia/West Bengal:** Uber roster includes Haldia, Haora and multiple river-adjacent West Bengal rows. Strong candidate for derivative riverine brief.

### Phase draft

- **Prove:** exact row extraction + sub-proposal prose for Goa, Mumbai, Kochi and Chennai.
- **Scale:** expand to Haldia/Haora, Vizag-area rows and other coastal Maharashtra/Gujarat/Tamil Nadu candidates where Atlas matches.
- **Mature:** economics after sealed corridors and crosswalk render QA.

### Guardrails

Keep global Uber intact. Build `uber-india` or equivalent draft namespace only after route/city IDs are safely extracted.
