## 4 · Posture classification
**Operate-posture, single-counterparty (dual in effect: RSG is both authority-equivalent and operator).** RSG already runs destination marine mobility (Coastal Transportation Services Co., 13 vessels), owns every landing, and has published commitments — 100% renewables, electric-only marine vehicles in the lagoon MSP, 30% net conservation gain — that its current conventional transfer fleet does not deliver. Per the international addendum, the pitch posture is **"extend your marine network"**: Navier is the marine mode that closes the gap between RSG's published environmental commitments and its existing guest-transfer operation (fast, silent, zero-emission, low-wake over reef/seagrass). Existing water-transit precedent is proven in-house: scheduled speedboat/yacht transfers from Turtle Bay jetty and seaplane operations already run daily — demand and the regulatory template both exist.
