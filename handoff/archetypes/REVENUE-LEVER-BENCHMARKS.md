## Gaps — failed closed

- **Uber Black tier for Salem→Boston and Hingham→Boston:** Uber's route pages publish only trailing-average prices for standard options; no Black-tier figure (only a 2016 forum screenshot — rejected as stale).
- **Boston-named medical courier base rates:** Boston firms (RTD Logistics, Couriers Boston) do not publish base pricing; only a national 2026 medical-courier rate guide (Dropoff) is citable.
- **Classic Harbor Line Boston and City Experiences Boston private-charter $/hr:** inquiry-only; City Experiences publishes only per-person budget bands ($50–$200+).
- **US ferry naming-rights or vessel-wrap deal with a disclosed dollar value:** none found; NYC Ferry's naming-rights sale is in process (agency selected 2025) with no value yet; Citi Bike's 2023 renewal value is undisclosed.
- **Waterborne freight unit economics:** DHL Amsterdam/London, Venice boats, and NYC Blue Highways all confirm programs exist but publish no per-parcel/per-run economics; Massport has no published urban waterborne freight program.
- **Commuter-ferry load factors (% of seats filled):** neither MBTA nor Seastreak publishes per-sailing load-factor stats; only ridership counts are public.
- **BHCC/MBTA adult 10-ride book price (Hingham):** City Experiences commuter page lists Adult Ten Ride as "N/A" (only Senior Ten Ride $48.50 shown).
- **MIT water shuttle:** no priced MIT-affiliated water shuttle exists (EZRide is a free land shuttle); Encore's water shuttle is free.
