# DEMAND-POOLS — Jeddah

Internal audit file. **All pools INDICATIVE** — they size context, not schedules. Fail closed: nothing here converts to ridership without a stated capture assumption in the model layer. Research date: 2026-08-16.

## 1 · Resident base
- Jeddah city population **3,751,722** (2022 Saudi Census; 11.6% of the Kingdom's population; second-largest city) — Saudipedia https://saudipedia.com/en/jeddah-city [SECONDARY citing PRIMARY census]. 2026 metro estimates ~3.8–4M [SECONDARY]. Indicative.
- Income context: Saudi-national average wage SAR 10,017–10,238/mo [PRIMARY GASTAT — see CREW-COST file]; Jeddah hosts the Kingdom's commercial/merchant elite and the corniche luxury strip. Indicative.

## 2 · Hajj/Umrah gateway (CONTEXT ONLY — not direct water-transit demand)
- King Abdulaziz International Airport: **53.4M passengers in 2025** (record; "Mega Airport" class), 310,000 flights, +12% bag growth YoY — kaia.sa press release (scraped) https://www.kaia.sa/News-and-Media/Press-releases-and-news/53-Million-Passenger [PRIMARY].
- Pilgrims transit Jeddah to Makkah (89 km) largely via Haramain High-Speed Rail (60M pax/yr capacity; station at KAIA) and road [SECONDARY citing Saudipedia]. **Label: gateway context — pilgrim flows are inland-bound and NOT modeled as water-transit demand.** A thin leisure slice (pre/post-Umrah corniche tourism) reaches the waterfront; unsized [UNSOURCED — record only].

## 4 · Jeddah Season (GEA — official)

## 5 · F1 Jeddah Corniche Circuit (March/April)
- 6.174 km street circuit on the corniche; hosted F1 annually since Dec 2021 — saudipedia.com/en/formula-one-races-in-jeddah [SECONDARY, official-adjacent]. Stand capacity ~50,000 [SECONDARY yachtcharterfleet]. JYC marina (~91 berths, yachts to 115–120 m) becomes F1 superyacht hub, 20+ superyachts race weekend [SECONDARY superyachttimes; jeddahyc.com PRIMARY existence]. Race-weekend water-shuttle + charter surge = event-anchored indicative pool.

## 6 · Jeddah Yacht Club & superyacht economy
- JYC & Marina: first Red Sea superyacht marina, ~91 berths, up to 115–120 m LOA, built 2021 for F1 — superyachttimes.com, dockwalk.com, burgessyachts.com [SECONDARY]; jeddahyc.com berth-holder guidebook [PRIMARY existence]. Water-taxi station live at JYC [PRIMARY-quality report]. Indicative premium-demand anchor + interchange node.

## 7 · Obhur Creek leisure economy
- Red Sea Marina (North Obhur): **300 berths** (3 jetties) + 108 shaded dry berths, yachts 8–60 m, est. 1987, 53,000 m² site 24 km north of Jeddah — redseamarina.com (scraped) [PRIMARY operator].
- Al Ahlam Marina (Obhur): active charter/leisure marina; **SRSA-licensed tourist-marina operator (first cohort, 2024, along with Red Sea Marina)** [SECONDARY citing SRSA].
- Dense private-marina/resort shoreline both banks; Visit Saudi promotes Obhur boat trips [PRIMARY existence]. Charter market clears at SAR 400–2,000/hr (see FARE file). Indicative.

## 8 · Durrat Al-Arus (north leisure)
