## Headline finding — Jeddah ALREADY OPERATES public water transit
**The Jeddah Water Taxi was officially inaugurated 6 March 2025 by Mayor Saleh Al-Turki**, run by the **Jeddah Transport Company (JTC)** (city transport arm) with local operator MACNA on trial basis. Phase 1 connects **Jeddah Yacht Club ↔ Historic Jeddah (Al-Balad, via the port) ↔ Obhur**; two vessels (94 pax + 55 pax, air-conditioned); fares SAR 25–50/trip, children ≤5 free; dedicated booking app (app.jedtc.com.sa); ceremony attended by the Deputy Minister of Transport & Logistic Services. — Arab News, 06 Mar 2025, https://www.arabnews.com/node/2592678/amp [PRIMARY-quality contemporaneous report; corroborated by TimeOut Jeddah https://www.timeoutjeddah.com/news/jeddah-water-taxi and multiple outlets, SECONDARY]
- JTC CEO Yousef Al-Sayegh: trial aims at "testing the service, gathering data, and understanding the market"; golf-cart agreement with Ministry of Culture for Al-Balad↔port last-mile. [same source]
- The **Jeddah Mayoralty plans up to 20 water-taxi stations** — Saudi Gazette (via its official FB post) https://www.facebook.com/SaudiGazette/posts/650596303914449 [SECONDARY citing the Mayoralty]
- **Posture consequence:** the city is in Operate mode with a stated expansion pipeline (3 → 20 stations). Navier's pitch is literally "extend your marine network": faster (25 kn vs displacement), zero-emission, and fills the network the Mayoralty already announced.

## 1 · Saudi Red Sea Authority (SRSA) — LEAD marine-tourism regulator (Enable)
- Established by Council of Ministers decision 30 Nov 2021 "as an enabler and regulator of marine and navigational tourism activities within the geographical scope of Saudi Arabia's Red Sea" — Wikipedia summarizing SRSA charter, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Red_Sea_Authority [SECONDARY citing PRIMARY]. **Scope confirmed: Red Sea coast → Jeddah is inside SRSA jurisdiction** (the inverse of the EP finding, where SRSA did not apply).
- Mandate (published): issuing licenses/permits for navigational & marine tourism activities; **identifying areas, routes, and navigational maps** for marine tourism "in coordination with relevant entities"; marine-environment protection; investment attraction & promotion. [SECONDARY citing SRSA]
- **Seven regulations issued 2023** (in force after 90 days): Private Visiting Yachts · Large Yacht Chartering · Tourist Navigational (Maritime Tourism) Agent · Cruise Ships · **Marinas Design & Operation** · Marine (Marina) Tourism Operator · **Marine Tourism Watercraft Classification** — U.S. ITA trade.gov market-intelligence note, https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/saudi-arabia-tourism-red-sea-authority-new-regulations-maritime-tourist [PRIMARY — US-gov summary]; regulation texts archived from redsea.gov.sa (e.g., Watercraft Classification: https://web.archive.org/web/20240224082545/https://redsea.gov.sa/en/node/167286) [PRIMARY archived].
- Licensing is live, in Jeddah specifically: SRSA's **first tourist-marina operator licenses (2024) went to Al Ahlam Marina (Jeddah), Red Sea Marina (Jeddah)** and Al Ahlam Marina (Jazan). [SECONDARY citing SRSA announcements]
- SRSA + 19 government entities produced **the first geographical navigation map of the Saudi Red Sea** (with GEOSA). [SECONDARY citing SRSA]
- Fit for Navier: the **Marine Tourism Watercraft Classification Regulation** is the front door for classifying a foiling electric passenger vessel for coastal-tourism operation; the **Marine Tour Operator** and **Maritime Tourism Agent** regulations cover the operating entity. SRSA's mandate language ("enabler," investment attraction, route identification) is an explicit invitation. Posture: **Enable — actively building the coastal-tourism economy Navier extends.**

## 2 · TGA — Transport General Authority (Enable, national)
- National regulator of passenger transport incl. maritime; licenses marine passenger-transport activity and marine units for commercial operation; runs maritime-sector compliance inspections (same framework verified in EP pass; national scope) — tga.gov.sa [PRIMARY via snippet; geo-blocked]. A scheduled water-transit service (vs pure tourism charter) sits with TGA alongside SRSA's tourism regime; JTC water-taxi inauguration attended by Deputy Minister of Transport confirms MOTLS/TGA engagement in Jeddah marine transit. [PRIMARY-quality report, Arab News above]

## 3 · Mawani / Jeddah Islamic Port (JIP) — port landlord (Enable, constraint-setter)
- JIP: **62 berths (2023), 12.5 km² area, 11.2 km quay length, max draft 16 m**; the Kingdom's largest Red Sea port; handles **24% of total passengers via Saudi ports**; >5,000 ship calls/yr — Wikipedia (citing Mawani) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeddah_Islamic_Port + Saudipedia https://saudipedia.com/en/jeddah-city [SECONDARY citing PRIMARY].
- Navigational channel ~14 mi long × 400 m wide; pilotage 24/7 — arabseaports.com/saudi-arabia-ports/jeddah-islamic [SECONDARY].
- **Cruise homeport**: Jeddah Islamic Port is Cruise Saudi's homeport; cruise terminal upgraded for Quantum-class ships — cruisesaudi.com/ports (scraped) [PRIMARY].
- Relevance: harbourmaster controls port waters at the south end of the network; the existing water-taxi station serving Al-Balad works via the port ("the easiest and fastest route was through the port" — JTC CEO), proving small passenger craft are already accommodated in port waters under coordination. Fairway crossing is the southern constraint (see SPEED-RULES).

## 4 · Jeddah Municipality (Amanah) + Jeddah Transport Company (Operate)
- Amanah develops and controls the corniche/waterfront: Jeddah Waterfront stages 4–5 built from 2017 at **SAR 800M**; Al-Nawras Island project (north corniche, 1,000 m long, 52,240 m², incl. a boat marina) — Saudipedia https://saudipedia.com/en/jeddah-city [SECONDARY citing PRIMARY].
- JTC (municipal) owns/operates the water taxi (above) and plans up to 20 stations. **This is the Operate-posture anchor entity for the PT page.**

## 5 · Jeddah Central Development Company (JCDC) — PIF giga-project (Enable, landing partner)

- Fit: JCDC's marina/pier is the natural mid-city station of a corniche water-transit spine from 2027+; PIF ownership aligns with the sovereign partner file (background only — do not render partner-file content).

## 7 · Border Guard (MOI) — safety/permits (national; same as EP)
- Regulations of Security & Safety for Maritime Activities: Border Guard determines navigational lanes for small craft, **their speeds**, departure/return times, sailing durations; issues sailing permits (text via Qanoniah legal DB) [PRIMARY-TEXT; national scope — applies on the Red Sea coast identically]. Sailing-permit negotiation is a launch-critical path item.

## City classification: DUAL — Operate-led
Jeddah is **dual, Operate-led**: the Municipality/JTC already operates water transit with a published 20-station ambition (Operate — "extend your marine network" posture per addendum), while SRSA (tourism vessels/marinas/routes), TGA (passenger transport), Mawani (port waters) and Border Guard (lanes/permits) form the Enable stack. JCDC and Amanah are landing-infrastructure partners. No corridor inventions: geometry authored fresh in NODES-JEDDAH.md (no Atlas corridor set exists for Jeddah).
