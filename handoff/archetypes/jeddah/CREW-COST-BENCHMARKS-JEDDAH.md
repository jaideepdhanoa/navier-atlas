## Electricity tariff — PRIMARY re-verification (national; applies in Jeddah)
- SERA (regulator) Consumption Tariff: Commercial 1–6,000 kWh/mo = 22 halalah/kWh; **>6,000 kWh/mo = 32 halalah/kWh** — https://www.sera.gov.sa/en/consumer/electric-tariff/electric-tariff-categories/consumption-tariff [PRIMARY — sera.gov.sa geo-blocks the sandbox; captured via search index of the primary page].
- SEC: "Commercial 1–6000 (kWh/Month) 22; More than 6000 (kWh/Month) 32 (Halalah/kWh)" — https://www.se.com.sa/en/Ourservices/ColumnC/Bills-and-Consumption/ConsumptionTariffs/ [PRIMARY, same capture method]. Tariff is national — SEC serves Jeddah (Western Operating Area); no Jeddah-specific commercial tariff deviation found.
- Corroboration: Saudi Gazette article 652853 [SECONDARY].
- A fleet charging point exceeds 6,000 kWh/mo → **SAR 0.32/kWh = $0.0853/kWh** [PRIMARY-derived]. This is ~72% below the $0.30/kWh canon default — per addendum rule, the cheaper sourced local tariff is used. SEC "intensive consumption tariff" (2025) may improve further for qualifying facilities [PRIMARY existence]; not assumed.
