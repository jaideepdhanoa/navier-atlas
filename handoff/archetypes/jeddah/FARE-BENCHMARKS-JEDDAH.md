## Read-through for pricing (feeds REVENUE-INPUTS)
1. Existing water transit prices at SAR 25–50 — slow, leisure-shaped, evening-hours. A 25-kn foiling express is a different product; price above (SAR 60–90 full-spine) yet below point-to-point ride-hail+time cost at peak.
2. The experience market clears at SAR 300–450/pp for 4–6-hr water days and SAR 2,000/hr for premium hulls — Jeddah's leisure wallet is deep and PROVEN (headline L3 layer).
3. Airport/pilgrim flows are road-locked (SAR 70–190, congestion) but land at an inland airport — water is not a direct airport substitute; do not model airport transfer revenue.
