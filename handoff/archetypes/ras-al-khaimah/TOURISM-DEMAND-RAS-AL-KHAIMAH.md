## 5 · Seasonality & honest shape

12-month operating year; **summer-heat midday shape** (Jun–Sep): midday dip, early-morning/evening peaks (addendum-mandated assumption, stated). Winter Oct–Apr is peak (Half Marathon 10K+ participants, UAE Tour, NYE fireworks/drone record along the coastline — all RAKTDA-published). Sheltered southern Gulf coast; shamal chop is the weather factor foiling flies above (brief canon). **Honest demand read:** today's base (1.35M visitors, ~50% domestic, 71% occupancy on ~8–9K keys) supports an experience/resort-transfer-led stack only; the commuter layer is thin until Wynn (2027, 9,000 staff + 1,530 keys) and Marjan Beach land. Conservative scenario in the stack is deliberately pre-Wynn and is **cash-negative** — reported, not massaged.
