# AUTHORITY-MAP-RAS-AL-KHAIMAH (internal audit file — never renders)

**As of:** 2026-08-16 · All URLs accessed 2026-08-16 · Peg 3.6725 AED/USD (canon).
**City classification: ENABLE+BUILD.** Unlike Dubai/Abu Dhabi, the authority is *building* marine transit, not operating it at scale: RAKTA's marine footprint today is a heritage abra service on the RAK Creek/Corniche with 3–4 stations. The legal machinery (Law 13/2023 + its Executive Regulation, May 2026) is brand new and expressly investment-facing. Posture: **"build the marine spine with the authority"** — co-development of a clean public water network from the start, not augmentation of an operating fleet. Domestic-first: RAK coastal corridors lead; any Dubai leg is later-phase roadmap only (pipeline-timing canon RAKTA 2029 must not be contradicted; microsites carry no launch dates).

**Disclosure rails:** "RAK Ports" is fine to use (the firewalled name is the Abu Dhabi three-letter ports group only). No royal-office references; Wynn Al Marjan Island is citable as a published development with sources.

## 2 · The legal instruments (citable, primary)

- **Law No. (13) of 2023 on Licensing and Operating Maritime Means in Ras Al Khaimah** — grants RAKTA the powers to license and regulate the emirate's marine transport system. Sources: rak.ae Legislation Search (text of legislation, https://www.rak.ae/wps/portal/rak/legislative-committee/legislation-search — search "Law No. (13) of 2023"); WAM: https://www.wam.ae/en/article/3ur2p3-rak-ruler-issues-law-licensing-operation-marine (scrape blocked this run; cited from search index — flag).
- **Executive Regulation of Law 13/2023** — issued by the Crown Prince/Executive Council Chairman, announced **20 May 2026** (RAKTA primary): designates RAKTA as implementing body; governs vessel categories and operational controls, visiting-vessel licensing, **"the organization of marinas and berthing facilities"**, maritime training centers, events; safety, technical **and environmental** specifications; fees; penalties; mandates integrated vessel/licensee databases. Source: https://www.rakta.gov.ae/news/mohammed-bin-saud-issues-the-executive-regulation-of-the-law-on-licensing-and-operating-marine-vessels-in-ras-al-khaimah/
- Quotable alignment language, H.E. Eng. Ismail Hassan Al Blooshi, Director General, RAKTA (same source): the Regulation *"will contribute to enhancing safety and operational efficiency… supporting digital transformation across various maritime procedures and services"* and *"supports the investment environment in the maritime sector by providing a clear and stable regulatory framework that boosts investor confidence and supports the growth of maritime transport, tourism, and water sports activities."* A zero-emission foiling network is that sentence made operational.
- **Act No. (9) of 2008 with respect to RAK Ports** (port regulations incl. pilotage, conduct, penalties): https://rakports.ae/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/rak-ports-regulation-2008-1.pdf
