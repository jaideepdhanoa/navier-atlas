# AUTHORITY-MAP-ABU-DHABI (internal audit file — never renders)

**As of:** 2026-08-16 · All URLs accessed 2026-08-16. Peg 3.6725 AED/USD (canon).
**City classification: OPERATE-dominant.** The emirate already runs public marine transit — ferries, water taxis, purpose-built marine terminals opened 2024–2025 — under a single integrated transport authority. Posture per INTERNATIONAL-ADDENDUM: **"extend your marine network"** (augmentation of an operating system, not a from-scratch enablement case). Domestic-first: emirate-internal corridors lead; inter-emirate appears as later-phase roadmap only.

**Disclosure note (hard firewall):** the maritime regulator/waterways custodian is referred to in every file, always and only, as **"Abu Dhabi Maritime."** No parent-group naming anywhere.

## 1 · Authority map

| Authority | Mandate | Controls | Posture fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DMT — Department of Municipalities & Transport** | Emirate-level municipalities + transport policy; issued the maritime safety bylaw (below) | Legislation, transport policy, marina berth-lease contract attestation | Policy umbrella; Operate-side sponsor |
| **ITC / "Abu Dhabi Mobility"** (Integrated Transport Centre, DMT affiliate) | The emirate's integrated transport authority — plans, regulates and operates public transport across land and marine modes | Public transit operations & franchising, taxis (incl. fares), Darb road tolling, marine transit development; co-implementing body of the maritime safety bylaw | **Operate — the primary counterpart.** Already commissions ferries and terminals; a foiling layer extends its marine network |
| **Abu Dhabi Maritime** | Single custodian of Abu Dhabi's waterways: waterway licensing, safety maps & zoned speed limits, public marine facilities (marinas, harbours), ferry & water-taxi operations | Landings (public marinas/terminals + published berth tariffs), navigation rules, vessel permits, day-to-day marine ops | **Operate + Enable** — operator of the existing network and gatekeeper of every landing |
| **DCT Abu Dhabi** (Culture & Tourism) | Destination strategy, hotel classification, cultural district (Saadiyat) | Visitor-economy programming, attraction development, tourism data | Enable — demand-side sponsor for the tourism-weighted layers |
| **EAD — Environment Agency Abu Dhabi** | Marine protected areas incl. Mangrove National Park | Protected-water access conditions, wildlife/wake rules | Enable — zero-wake/zero-emission operation is the permitting fit for mangrove waters |

## 2 · The legal instrument (citable)

- **Regulatory Bylaw for Maritime Safety in the Waterways of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi** — announced by DMT 11 July 2025; effective on Official Gazette publication. Referenced as **Administrative Decision No. (69) of 2025** in Abu Dhabi Maritime's published marina tariff schedule (§2.2.10). Establishes licensing, operational conduct, environmental obligations, emergency duties; "Abu Dhabi Maritime, in collaboration with the Integrated Transport Centre – a DMT affiliate – will serve as the implementing body for many of the regulations."
  Source: https://admobility.gov.ae/en/news/comprehensive-maritime-safety-regulations-to-safeguard-waterways-and-support-sustainable-growth
- Quotable plan language, Dr Abdulla Hamad AlGhfeli, Acting Director General, ITC: *"Through this integrated regulatory framework, we aim to improve the efficiency of maritime operations, safeguard the environment, promote the safe and sustainable use of our waterways… to support our vision for a safer and more sustainable future."* (same source)

## 4 · Alignment language for the proposal

- The authority's own frame is **network extension + sustainability**: AlGhfeli on the Dalma vessels — *"…reflect our commitment to delivering sustainable, efficient, and innovative transport solutions… to advance Abu Dhabi's position as a leading maritime hub."* (admaritime Dalma release, above). A zero-emission foiling layer is the same sentence continued.
- Green-mobility direction: a published roadmap aims to transform Abu Dhabi Island into a green public-transport zone by 2030 (secondary/social source: https://www.facebook.com/dctabudhabi/posts/1303021435183464 — label as secondary if ever referenced; do not render as a commitment).
- Environmental law hook: the 2025 bylaw "places a special emphasis on marine environmental protection" (DMT announcement, §2 source) — zero-wake, zero-emission craft are the compliance-native fleet for mangrove and protected waters (see SPEED-RULES file).
- Pipeline-timing canon (ITC 2028) must not be contradicted; microsites carry no launch dates.
