## 5 · Seasonality & shape

12-month operating year; **summer-heat midday shape** (Jun–Sep): demand dips midday, shifts to evening peaks (all L3 night products in market: full-moon/night-glow mangrove kayaks, evening cruises); winter Oct–Apr is the peak window (F1, events). Assumption stated per addendum; sheltered island/mangrove waters operable year-round.
