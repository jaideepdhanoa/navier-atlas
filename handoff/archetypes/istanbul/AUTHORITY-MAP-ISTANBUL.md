## Framing rule (non-negotiable)
Istanbul has one of the world's great ferry systems. Navier NEVER frames itself as introducing water transit here. The honest posture: a zero-emission, low-wake EXPRESS/premium overlay above an already-excellent public network — plus alignment with İBB's published 2050 carbon-neutral plan.

## 1 · İstanbul Büyükşehir Belediyesi (İBB) — Metropolitan Municipality
- **Mandate:** Metropolitan-scale public transport planning and coordination under Law 5216 (Büyükşehir Belediyesi Kanunu); owns the city ferry operator (Şehir Hatları A.Ş.) and the sea-taxi service; UKOME (Ulaşım Koordinasyon Merkezi) under İBB is the single body that sets routes, tariffs and issues line permissions for intra-city passenger transport, including sea routes. Evidence: UKOME decision records covering "Hat İzin Belgesi" (line permit) issuance and tariff decisions for sea transport. [PRIMARY — UKOME decision PDF via İSTESOB: https://www.istesob.org.tr/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/30.11.2022-ve-26.01.2023-tarihli-UKOME-kararlari-30.03.2023.pdf]
- **Climate mandate:** İstanbul Climate Change Action Plan — carbon-neutral 2050, with published interim absolute-reduction targets: −52% by 2030, −89% by 2040. [PRIMARY — İBB Environment Directorate: https://cevre.ibb.istanbul/iklim-degisikligi-sube-mudurlugu/2050de-karbon-notr-ve-direncli-bir-istanbul-icin-yol-haritamiz-hazir/]
- **TUHİM (İBB Toplu Ulaşım Hizmetleri Müdürlüğü):** administers transport permits/ruhsat processes incl. marine tourism permits ("turizm ruhsatı" application forms published). [PRIMARY — https://tuhim.ibb.gov.tr]
- **Posture fit:** the ENABLE-or-OPERATE partner. Operate variant: Navier vessels into Şehir Hatları/İBB service (like the sea-taxi program İBB already runs). Enable variant: UKOME line permit + tariff approval for a Navier-operated premium express tier integrated with İstanbulkart.

## 2 · Şehir Hatları A.Ş. — İBB's city ferry operator
- **Scale (their own words):** "53 iskele, 30 vapurla günde ortalama 903 sefer… yılda 40 milyon civarında yolcu" — 53 piers, 30 ferries, ~903 daily sailings, ~40M pax/yr. [PRIMARY — https://sehirhatlari.istanbul/tr/kurumsal/hakkimizda-215]
- Operates Bosphorus crossings, Golden Horn line, Adalar (Princes' Islands) lines, Bosphorus tours. Publishes pier list and fleet pages. [PRIMARY — sehirhatlari.istanbul/tr/iskeleler, /tr/gemiler]
- **Fit:** the natural operating partner and pier landlord. Their piers are the landing inventory; a foiling express tier can sell as a premium product above the vapur network without touching its fares or coverage.

## 3 · National layer — UAB and its maritime arms
- **UAB (Ministry of Transport & Infrastructure), Denizcilik Genel Müdürlüğü:** vessel registration/flag, survey and certification, seafarer certification; İstanbul Liman Başkanlığı (Harbor Master, under UAB) administers the port area and publishes the **Yerel Deniz Trafiği Rehberi** (Local Marine Traffic Guide) that governs local traffic conduct, zones and speed. [PRIMARY — İstanbul Liman Başkanlığı, Yerel Deniz Trafiği Rehberi PDF (denizmevzuat.uab.gov.tr / liman başkanlığı publication)]
- **Turkish Straits regime:** Türk Boğazları Deniz Trafik Düzeni Yönetmeliği (Presidential Decree No. 1426, RG 2019; successor of the 1998 regulation) — governs Bosphorus traffic incl. the 10-kn speed article (see SPEED-RULES file). [PRIMARY — mevzuat.gov.tr text of Karar 1426]
- **Kıyı Emniyeti Genel Müdürlüğü (Directorate General of Coastal Safety):** operates the Turkish Straits Vessel Traffic Services (TBGTH/VTS), pilotage, salvage; VTS announces current/closure conditions. [PRIMARY — kiyiemniyeti.gov.tr; VTS role also codified in Karar 1426, e.g., Art. 36(3)]
- **Fit:** UAB layer = vessel/class/crew certification and harbor-master conduct rules; it is NOT the route-allocation body for intra-city lines (that is UKOME/İBB). New-technology (foiling) vessel acceptance will run through UAB flag/survey — flag this as a program workstream.

[SECONDARY — ido.com.tr self-description (PRIMARY for current route offer); privatization widely documented — VERIFY exact ownership before rendering]
- **Dentur Avrasya:** private operator since 2002; Bosphorus crossings (e.g., Beşiktaş–Üsküdar), Bosphorus tours, Islands tours; an İSTKA (Istanbul Development Agency) study records ~40 motorized boats and ~50,000 pax/day. [SECONDARY — İSTKA: https://www.istka.org.tr/media/20863/… "İstanbul'da deniz ulaşımının geleceğinin değerlendirilmesi"; PRIMARY for services — denturavrasya.com]

- **Mavi Marmara** and smaller motor operators also run licensed local lines. [SECONDARY — İSTKA study]

Proves İBB already operates a PREMIUM on-demand water tier. [SECONDARY — 2026 tariff reporting (eleman.net tariff tracker); VERIFY at deniztaksi.istanbul before rendering figures]

## 6 · City classification
**Dual, İBB-led → classify OPERATE-first with an ENABLE fallback.** İBB both operates (Şehir Hatları, Deniz Taksi) and licenses (UKOME). The strongest wedge is with İBB itself: a foiling express/premium tier inside the İBB family (Şehir Hatları premium product or Deniz Taksi scale-up), consistent with the 2050 carbon-neutral plan. The Enable path (UKOME permit to a Navier-operated line) exists — İDO/Dentur/Turyol precedents prove private/mixed operation is normal on these waters.
