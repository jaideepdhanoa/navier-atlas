# SPEED-RULES-ISTANBUL — the critical file
Internal research record — audit language, never renders. Date: 2026-08-16.

## HEADLINE DETERMINATION
**The 10-kn limit is real, applies over-ground, and covers the ENTIRE Bosphorus area including local (city) traffic — not just transiting ships. High-speed craft are additionally and explicitly capped at 10 kn north of the İnci Burnu–Ahırkapı line. South of that line — the Sea of Marmara — there is NO blanket speed cap, only COLREG safe-speed and specific small zones. Therefore: Bosphorus express economics are DEAD at foiling speed under current rules; Marmara trunk and Princes' Islands expresses are FULLY VIABLE at 25 kn.**

## 1 · Turkish Straits transit regime (national)
- **Türk Boğazları Deniz Trafik Düzeni Yönetmeliği** (Presidential Decree No. 1426, 2019; successor to the 1998 regulation):
  - **MADDE 14(1):** "Gemilerin İstanbul ve Çanakkale Boğazlarından geçiş hızı, karaya göre saatte 10 deniz milidir." — transit speed through the Istanbul and Çanakkale Straits is 10 knots over the ground. [PRIMARY — regulation text (mevzuat.gov.tr / lexpera transcription of Karar 1426)]
  - **MADDE 36 (currents):** when the surface current exceeds 4 kn (or orkoz counter-currents form), low-maneuverability/dangerous-cargo/large/deep-draft vessels must wait; above 6 kn they may not enter regardless of speed. VTS (TBGTH) announces current states. [PRIMARY — same text]
- Scope note: Article 14 is written for vessels in passage ("geçiş"). The local-traffic layer is governed by the harbor master's guide below — which imposes the same 10-kn ceiling on everyone inside the strait.

## 2 · Local traffic regime — İstanbul Liman Başkanlığı, Yerel Deniz Trafiği Rehberi
[PRIMARY — Local Marine Traffic Guide, İstanbul Harbor Master (UAB). All quotes verbatim from the PDF.]
- **Blanket cap:** "İstanbul Boğazı sınırları içerisinde gemilerin azami hızı karaya göre 10 deniz milidir." — within the boundaries of the İstanbul Strait the maximum speed of vessels is 10 kn over the ground. Applies to local traffic, not only transit.
- **High-speed craft rule:** vessels designed for high speed with poor low-speed maneuverability (deniz otobüsleri named as the type) "İnci Burnu ile Ahırkapı Feneri arasında çizilen hattın kuzeyinde kalan bölgede 10 milden fazla sürat yapmayacaklardır" — may not exceed 10 kn NORTH of the line drawn between İnci Burnu (Kadıköy breakwater light) and Ahırkapı Lighthouse (Sarayburnu south). **Implication (DERIVED, corroborated by decades of seabus operation): south of that line, in the Marmara, high-speed craft may run at service speed**, subject to COLREG safe speed and the wash duty below.
- **Wash duty outside the strait:** the same craft "İstanbul Boğazı sınırları dışında seyir veya yanaşma esnasında, oluşan dalgalardan etkilenecek teknelerin olması durumunda hızlarını azaltacaklardır" — outside strait limits they must reduce speed when their wake would affect other craft. This is the codified wake rule — purpose-built for exactly what a low-wake foiler solves. [PRIMARY]
- **Noise:** local-traffic vessels in the Bosphorus and Golden Horn must avoid noise above **85 dB**. [PRIMARY]
- **Kadıköy/Moda zones (affects IST-1/IST-2 approaches):**
  - Kalamış–Fenerbahçe marina entry corridor (defined by 40°58.5'N 029°02.0'E / Moda Burnu bearings): **max 4 kn both directions**. [PRIMARY]
  - Area east/inside of the Moda Burnu 180° line to 40°58'N (Moda bay): motorized craft **max 4 kn**. [PRIMARY]
- **Golden Horn (Haliç):** when the Galata/Unkapanı bridges are open, no crossing between Sirkeci–Karaköy quays and the bridges; Haliç-only vessels have specific exemptions; 85 dB noise rule applies. No separate numeric speed limit captured in this pass beyond the Bosphorus-area cap — treat Haliç as ≤10 kn AND wake-sensitive (see §5). [PRIMARY for the rules quoted; Haliç-specific numeric limit UNSOURCED — fail closed]

## 3 · Currents (schedule math input)
- The guide: complex currents reaching **6–7 kn** in places; winter current up to **6–8 kn** with strong orkoz (southerly-wind counter-current) episodes; typical northern surface current runs southbound. [PRIMARY — Rehber]
- Regulation Art. 36 uses 4 kn and 6 kn as operational thresholds — i.e., ≥4 kn surface current is a normal, recurring condition. [PRIMARY]
- Schedule consequence: inside the strait the cap is over-ground, so timetables are current-neutral but power demand is asymmetric. On Marmara legs currents are materially weaker (no strait jet); apply a ±1 kn allowance on Islands legs (DERIVED assumption, flagged).

## 5 · Wake damage — the documented relief case
- **PRIMARY:** İstanbul Liman Başkanlığı letter dated 02.12.2024, No. 2285999 (circulated by the Turkish Chamber of Shipping/DTO): high-speed craft navigating the Golden Horn create large waves that have caused "serious damage" (ciddi hasarlar) to moored vessels; masters to be continuously warned. Source: DTO circular republishing the harbor master letter (denizticaretodasi.org.tr).
- **Context:** ~620 Ottoman-era yalıs (wooden waterfront mansions) line the Bosphorus [SECONDARY — Wikipedia/Bosphorus & Yalı articles]; academic literature documents amplification of ship-generated wake wash by coastal effects [SECONDARY — e.g., ResearchGate: "Amplification of ship generated wake wash due to coastal effects"]. No single primary study quantifying yalı wake damage was captured — do NOT render a quantified yalı-damage claim; render only: wake damage from fast craft is a documented, harbor-master-level concern in these waters (primary), and the low-wake foiler serves the rule's own purpose.
- **"What relief unlocks" (labeled, never base case):** the HSC 10-kn rule and the wash-reduction duty exist to control wake and collision risk from craft that maneuver poorly at low speed. A foiling vessel at speed produces a fraction of displacement-craft wake and handles conventionally at low speed — the rule's stated purposes (wake, noise ≤85 dB, safety) are served better, not worse. Any Bosphorus speed relief would be an İBB/UKOME + UAB/Liman Başkanlığı conversation. Base schedule math NEVER assumes it.
