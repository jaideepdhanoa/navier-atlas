## 1 · Population & macro

- Two continents, one strait: the entire east–west commute economy crosses the Bosphorus or rides the Marmara shore.

## 2 · Cross-Bosphorus travel (indicative)
- **Marmaray (rail tunnel): ~607,000 pax/day average, 2025**; Metrobüs ~748,000/day. [SECONDARY — İBB-reported 2025 daily averages via press]
- Rail share (incl. Marmaray) of İstanbulkart journeys rose from 35.5% (2018) to **42.9% (2025)**. [SECONDARY — İBB reporting]
- Bridge road crossings: commonly cited in the hundreds of thousands of vehicles/day per bridge — NOT captured from a primary source this pass → **UNSOURCED, excluded**. Total cross-strait person-trips "millions/day" renders only as INDICATIVE.
- **Maritime share is small and that is the story:** ~40M Şehir Hatları pax/yr ≈ ~110K/day [PRIMARY — sehirhatlari.istanbul hakkımızda] + private motors (Dentur alone ~50K/day per İSTKA study [SECONDARY]) vs ~1.3M+/day on Marmaray+Metrobüs alone → water carries a low-single-digit share of cross-strait trips despite piers everywhere. An express tier grows the water share; it does not cannibalize the vapur.

## 3 · Maritime ridership baseline
- **Şehir Hatları: 53 piers, 30 vessels, ~903 sailings/day, ~40M pax/yr.** [PRIMARY — hakkımızda page]
- Private operators (İSTKA "Future of Sea Transport in Istanbul" study, SECONDARY): Turyol ~65 motors; Dentur Avrasya ~40 motors, ~50K pax/day; Mavi Marmara also licensed. Total maritime ridership across all operators: not captured as one primary number → INDICATIVE "well above Şehir Hatları's 40M alone".

## 4 · Tourism (indicative, strong for L3/experience layer)
- **Türkiye 2025: 65.23B USD tourism revenue; 63.9M visitors** (TÜİK Tourism Statistics Q4-2025 bulletin). [PRIMARY — TÜİK veriportali press/54158]
- **İstanbul 2025: 17.478M foreign visitors — #1 in Türkiye with 34.9% share** [SECONDARY — Ministry of Culture & Tourism year-end data via press; 9-month official statement: 14.158M (Minister Ersoy, AA, PRIMARY-adjacent); monthly PRIMARY PDFs at istanbul.ktb.gov.tr]
- **Cruise 2025: İstanbul ports 625K+ cruise pax** (Ministry data; Türkiye total ~2.5M, record year; Galataport's own "record year" comms). [SECONDARY — seabusinessworld/NTV citing Ministry; Galataport PRIMARY comms available for re-pin]
- Bosphorus tour/charter demand is the visible tip: 6,000–8,000 TL/hr charters clear the market daily (see FARE file §4).

## 5 · Waterfront geography (honest map)
- **On the water:** Karaköy/Galataport (cruise + offices + hotels), Eminönü/Sirkeci (historic core), Kabataş (interchange to tram/funicular; Dolmabahçe hotels), Beşiktaş (offices + ferries), Üsküdar (Asian interchange), Kadıköy (Asian CBD-adjacent, huge rail/ferry interchange), Kalamış/Fenerbahçe (marina, 4-kn corridor), Bostancı (pier + İDO legacy terminal, east Asian side), Maltepe/Kartal/Pendik corridor (dense residential + business, piers exist), Yenikapı (rail superhub: Marmaray + metro; ferry terminal), Bakırköy/Ataköy (west residential + Ataköy Marina + offices).
- **Inland, honestly:** Levent/Maslak (the main CBD) and Ümraniye/Kozyatağı office districts are NOT on the water. Water access serves them via interchanges (Kabataş→metro/M6/M2 via transfer, Beşiktaş→bus/metro, Kadıköy→M4/Marmaray, Yenikapı→M1/M2/Marmaray). Never claim door-to-door CBD service.
- **Marinas (charter/experience + berthing):** Ataköy Marina (Europe west), Kalamış-Fenerbahçe (Asia, largest), West İstanbul Marina (Beylikdüzü), Viaport Marina (Tuzla). [SECONDARY — operator sites; capacities not captured → not used]

## 6 · Princes' Islands (Adalar)
- Car-free islands; access is water-only — Şehir Hatları Adalar lines + private motors (Bostancı/Kartal/Maltepe/Kabataş departures). Seasonal surge: summer weekends multiply traffic (fare tiers and dense summer schedules evidence the pattern). Annual visitation total: **UNSOURCED this pass → INDICATIVE only** ("millions annually" not to be rendered as a number).
- Express case: existing Kadıköy/Kabataş↔Büyükada services take ~50–100 min (INDICATIVE, re-verify schedules); a 25-kn foiler does Kadıköy→Büyükada in ~26 min (see NODES file) — a categorical time win, year-round resident + weekender + tourist mix.
