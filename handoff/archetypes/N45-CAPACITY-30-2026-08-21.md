# N45 capacity update — 20 → 30 seats (2026-08-21)

## Scope
Fleet-investor, public-partners, and employer-hub surfaces: N45 Explorer rated **30 passengers** (was 20). N30 remains 8.

## Financial method (FI interactive P&L)
Seat-driven `revenue_build` scenario rows scaled by **1.5× (30/20)**:
- Committed `N seats/mo`
- Spot `~N legs/mo` (spare-capacity derived)
- Experiences `sailings × seats` (per-sailing seats capped at 30)

**Not scaled:** charter hours, sponsorship, overnight cargo, opex bands, capex ($2.5M), network share %.

Scenario **gross / net / payback** recomputed as:
`net = gross × (1 − share%) − opex` with opex at lo / mid / hi band by scenario;  
`payback = $2.5M / (net × 12)` when net > 0.

|---|---|---|

| Mid payback | ~7.9 yr | **~3.4 yr** |
