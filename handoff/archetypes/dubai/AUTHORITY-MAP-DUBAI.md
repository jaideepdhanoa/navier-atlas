# AUTHORITY-MAP-DUBAI (internal audit file — never renders)

Accessed dates: all URLs loaded 2026-08-16 unless noted. Currency: AED cited, USD at peg 3.6725 (canon).

## Classification: primarily OPERATE (RTA is an operator with a published expansion mandate)

Dubai is the clearest OPERATE market in the pipeline. The RTA already runs a multi-modal marine network (abra, water bus, water taxi, Dubai Ferry) carrying 18.4M riders/yr and has a published Marine Transport Master Plan 2030 that commits to growing stations, lines, and fleet. The page posture is **"extend your marine network"** — Navier adds a fast, zero-emission foiling tier on corridors the RTA has already declared it wants to serve. Enable-side actors (DMA licensing, Municipality landings) exist but are secondary.

## 1. RTA — Roads and Transport Authority (Public Transport Agency / Marine Transport)

**Mandate:** Emirate-level transit authority (Law No. 17 of 2005, cited in ECR 9/2020 preamble). Plans, operates, and sets fares for all public transport including marine modes.

**Controls:** marine transit operations, routes, fares, marine stations (48 today), fleet procurement, service concessions.

**Existing marine operations (proof of demand):**
- Marine transport (abras, water taxis, Dubai Ferry) served **18.4M riders in 2025, +3% YoY** — Dubai Media Office (primary): https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/february/15-02/rta-records-802-riders-in-dubai-2025 . Total RTA network 802.1M riders 2025 (+7.4%).
- 2024 marine ridership 17.43M (+9%) — mediaoffice.ae, carried in `_ref-brief-dubai-uae.json` sources; NOT re-verified this run.
- Modes & published fares (RTA primary, https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/marine/about-marine):
  - Water taxi (Marina/Bluewaters BM routes): **AED 7 per stop, AED 7–21 full route**; unlimited day pass AED 25 (RTA social announcement; secondary capture, low confidence).
  - Heritage petrol abra, Dubai Water Canal: **AED 25/person/hour; AED 300/hour full-vessel charter**.
  - Dubai Ferry: 98-seat vessels; marine fares across modes range **AED 1–75 per person** (RTA service page, https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/rta-services/service-details?serviceId=358). Ferry Gold-class legs at the AED 75 top of that band (route-level fare table not primary-captured this run — see TOURISM file).
- On-demand charter of RTA water taxi ~AED 400/hr for up to 20 pax — secondary (dubaivisitsvisa.com); NOT primary-verified.

**Citable plan language — Marine Transport Master Plan 2030** (approved by the Crown Prince/Executive Council Chairman; WAM: https://www.wam.ae/en/article/hszrhvr5-hamdan-bin-mohammed-approves-dubai-marine ; full text loaded via Robban Assafina reproduction: https://www.assafinaonline.com/en/article/hamdan-bin-mohammed-approves-dubai-marine-transport-master-plan-2030):
- Riders: **14.7M → 22.2M/yr by 2030 (+51%)**
- Network length: **55 km → 158 km (+188%)**
- Stations: **48 → 79** (Creek, Water Canal, Gulf coastline, waterfront projects)
- Passenger lines: **7 → 35 (+400%)**
- Fleet: **196 → 258 vessels (+32%)**
- Includes a 20-passenger **3D-printed electric abra** program (explicit electrification signal) and the Al Garhoud marine maintenance centre (region's largest, 5,000 m², 16-vessel capacity).

**Posture fit:** OPERATOR. The plan needs +79 km of network, +28 lines, +62 vessels by 2030 — a fast foiling tier is additive capacity toward their own published targets, above the abra/water-bus fleet, without touching the heritage abra trade. Fares are already tiered (AED 1 abra → AED 75 ferry Gold), so a premium tier extends an existing fare ladder rather than inventing one.

## 2. DMA — Dubai Maritime Authority (formerly Dubai Maritime City Authority/DMCA), under PCFC

**Current name verified:** "the Dubai Maritime Authority (DMA), part of the Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation (PCFC)" — Dubai Media Office, 21 Aug 2025 (primary): https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/august/21-08/dma-launches-visitors-license . Historic basis: Law No. 11 of 2007 establishing Dubai Maritime City (DMCA affiliated to PCFC): https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20(11)%20of%202007%20Establishing.html . Legacy "DMCA" still appears in legislation and e-services (eservices.dmca.ae).

**Mandate & controls (primary — Executive Council Resolution No. 9 of 2020 amending ECR 11/2013, implementing Law 11/2010 on Licensing of Vessels; full text loaded: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2020/Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(9)%20of%202020%20Amending%20Executive%20Council%20Resolution%20No.%20(11)%20of%202013.html):**
- Licenses and registers all vessels in Emirate waters; four categories incl. **Commercial Vessels → Passenger Vessels**; "Group Maritime Transport" (group passenger transport for a fare) is a defined, licensable activity.
- Permits Masters/Seamen (Commercial Vessel Master's Permit AED 900; crew licensing manual updated per Administrative Resolution No. 5 of 2024 — mediaoffice Aug 2025 above).
- Art. 52: DMCA/DMA (with concerned entities) **"determine[s] the main routes of the Maritime Transport network… determine[s] authorised speed limits"**, manages anchorage/berthing areas, and regulates harbours — i.e., the DMA is the routing + speed + landing regulator; the RTA is the transit operator.
- Insurance from a UAE-licensed insurer is a licence prerequisite (Art. 9.8).

**Posture fit:** ENABLE-side regulator. Zero-emission compliance posture is straightforward (fine schedule penalizes non-low-sulphur fuel; electric platforms moot the issue).

## 3. Dubai Municipality

**Mandate/controls:** public beaches and coastline management via its Coastal Management Section (shoreline monitoring/management program documented in DHI "Shoreline Management of the Dubai Coast" — secondary: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/330451523_Shoreline_Management_of_the_Dubai_Coast); public beach expansion plan targeting **+400% public beach length** (secondary: https://water.fanack.com/dubai-smart-waterway-mapping-project/). Relevance: beach-adjacent landings and any new public-realm marine access points touch DM. Numeric plan details NOT primary-verified — treat as context only.

## 4. DET — Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism

**Mandate:** tourism economy planning and promotion; hotel licensing/classification.

**Sourced facts (visitor economy — feeds FI page):**
- **19.59M international overnight visitors in 2025 (+5% YoY, third record year)** — DET (primary): https://www.dubaidet.gov.ae/en/newsroom/press-releases/dubais-tourism-industry-achieves-third-successive-record-breaking-year
- Hotel inventory **154,264 rooms across 827 establishments** (end-Dec 2025), ahead of Bangkok/New York/Paris/Singapore — same DET release.
- H1-2025: 9.88M visitors; hotel occupancy 80.6%, 22.24M room nights — Dubai Media Office/DET: https://dmo.dof.gov.ae/en/news-and-publications/latest-press-releases/dubai-welcomes-988-million-international-visitors-in-the-first-half-of-2025/

**Posture fit:** demand-side validator for the tourism-weighted FI page; not a permitting authority for corridors.
