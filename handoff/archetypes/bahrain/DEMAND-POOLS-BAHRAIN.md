## 1 · National-scale flows (the headline signals)

### 1.1 King Fahd Causeway — the flagship congestion/demand signal
- GASTAT (Saudi official statistics) 2024 Road Transport Statistics: **65.9M passengers crossed Saudi land borders in 2024; King Fahd Causeway = 49.9% of all traffic → ≈ 32.9M passengers/yr ≈ ~90K/day average**. Source: GASTAT release reported by Saudi Gazette https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/656341 — **SECONDARY (news report of official statistics; pull GASTAT PDF for render)**.
- Consistent with the ref-file's "~33M crossings" claim — now re-verified against a 2024 official statistic (ref-file citation was a data-clean brief).
- KFCA (bilateral authority) publishes live transit times and runs expansion works — https://kfca.sa/en/aboutus — **PRIMARY**. Weekend/holiday peaks produce multi-hour queues (widely reported; specific queue-time figures UNSOURCED — do not quantify in render).

## 3 · Event & seasonal spikes
- **F1 Bahrain Grand Prix (Sakhir, ~April):** record **~105,000 weekend attendance / ~37,700 race-day (2025)**, 55% international visitors. Source: bahraingp.com / BIC press — **PRIMARY (circuit)**. Circuit is inland (no landing); demand expresses as hotel/airport/waterfront surge, not a route.
- **Ramadan:** demand shifts to post-iftar evening/night; combined with the MOI night-ban question this is a genuine scheduling tension — flag (DERIVED shape, see REVENUE-INPUTS).
- **Summer (Jun–Sep):** extreme heat suppresses midday leisure; Gulf-standard evening peak (DERIVED shape per addendum).
- **Qatar ferry (Nov 2025)** creates a new international passenger flow through **Sa'ada Marina** — an interchange pool for the domestic network. SECONDARY.
