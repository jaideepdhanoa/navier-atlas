## 1 · Authority landscape

### 1.1 Ministry of Transportation & Telecommunications (MTT) — the anchor authority
- **Mandate:** MTT's Ports and Maritime Affairs (PMA) "plays a pivotal role in regulating, developing, and promoting the ports and maritime industry." Source: https://www.mtt.gov.bh/ports-and-maritime — **PRIMARY**.
- **What it controls:** all ports, docks, harbours, piers, wharves, **private jetties**, fairways and free zones (except military/security facilities), under Legislative Decree No. (61) of 2006 (Ports and Marine Navigation Affairs Law). The Minister of Transportation issues the implementing regulations and can proclaim any facility a port. Law text: https://bahrainbusinesslaws.com/laws/Ports-and-Marine-Navigation-Law — **PRIMARY-TEXT** (unofficial reproduction of the law; official gazette text at lloc.gov.bh if needed).
- **Licensing of operators:** "Licence: a licence issued by the Ports and Marine Navigation Affairs to operate, manage or provide services at any Port" (Law 61/2006, Art. 1) — a scheduled Navier service and any new landing both run through PMA licensing. **PRIMARY-TEXT**.
- **Small-vessel regime:** Small Ship Registration Office registers/regulates all vessels **under 150 GT** under Royal Decree No. (32) of 2020 (Law on Small Ship Registration and Determination of Safety Conditions): registration, renewals, inspections, master licences, navigation licences for commercial and passenger boats. Source: https://www.mtt.gov.bh/small-ship-registration — **PRIMARY**. Online portal: ssrp.marasi.gov.bh.
- **Navigation permits for crew:** it is "prohibited by law to maneuver any boat registered under the Bahraini Flag without obtaining a valid navigation permit"; permits for captains/sailors on non-commercial vessels <105 GT via national portal. Source: https://services.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BSP/GSX-UI-EServiceDetails?esID=2090 — **PRIMARY**.
- **Visiting vessels:** Visiting Vessel Navigation Permit "grants authorization to sail and dock within Bahraini waters." https://www.mtt.gov.bh/projects/visiting-vessel-navigation-permit — **PRIMARY**.
- **Multimodal ambition (their own words/plans):** Bahrain Metro Phase 1 — 29 km, 20 stations, two lines (airport↔Seef among them). Source: http://www.mtt.gov.bh/projects/bahrain-metro — **PRIMARY**. MTT also regulates land transport (taxi tariffs published at mtt.gov.bh/taxis-fares — PRIMARY) and civil aviation.
- **Posture fit:** **Operate-adjacent Enable.** MTT regulates and licenses; the live water taxi is run by a private operator (Masar Group) under this framework. Navier's entry point: vessel/technology partner to the ministry's marine-transport modernization, plus PMA licensing for routes and landings.

### 1.2 Masar Group — the existing water-transport operator (proof of mode, and the ticketing rail)
- **Water Taxi Project "launched in April 2025"** — "a water-based transportation system … integrating water transport with existing public transit networks." Six stations: **Sa'ada · Manama East Coast · Water Garden City · Harbour House · The Avenues · Four Seasons**. Operator states **4K+ trips operated**; fleet listed as 1 passenger boat (28 pax, air-conditioned). Source: https://masargroup.bh/what-we-do/masar-transportation/ — **PRIMARY (operator)**.
- Fares (BTEA tourism portal): BHD 0.8/stop standard, BHD 1.5 VIP, BHD 2.5 / 5.0 day passes; 28 pax (20 standard + 8 VIP); Sun–Thu 14:30–21:45, Fri–Sat 12:00–24:00. Source: https://www.bahrain.com/en/experience-bahrain-by-water-taxi — **PRIMARY (official tourism portal)**.
- **Qatar–Bahrain passenger ferry** launched 6 Nov 2025: Al-Ruwais Port (QA) ↔ **Sa'ada Marina** (BH), ~35 nm, 70–80 min, booked via the **Masar app**, from ~QAR 174 / BHD 18 one-way. Sources: https://www.iloveqatar.net/guide/transportation/qatar-bahrain-ferry-prices-how-to-book ; dohanews/bahrainthismonth posts — **SECONDARY** (multiple consistent; operator booking channel PRIMARY = Masar app).
- Implication for proposal: the Masar app is the existing booking/ticketing rail — same integration story as the reference MOTC proposal. Do **not** copy the ref-file's claim set without this re-verification (done here).

### 1.3 Bahrain Tourism & Exhibitions Authority (BTEA)
- Established 2015; **licenses and regulates tourism businesses** (hotels, tour operators, guides) and runs tourism promotion; e-services portal confirms "Tourism Licensing & Services." Source: https://portal.btea.bh/MainP/AboutUs — **PRIMARY**. Enabling law: Law amended 2022 (Lexis Middle East note) — **SECONDARY**.
- Controls: tourism-experience licensing (relevant to L3 experience products), tourism data (Inbound Tourism Survey feeding data.gov.bh datasets — PRIMARY), destination marketing (bahrain.com).
- Posture fit: **Enable** — licensing + promotion partner for the visitor-economy layer; not a landings owner.

### 1.4 Ports operators / port-level bodies
- **Khalifa Bin Salman Port (KBSP)** — the Kingdom's main deep-sea port, operated by APM Terminals Bahrain under PMA supervision; MTT launched a new **vessel traffic management system (VTMS)** at KBSP (MTT news item on PMA page). Sources: https://www.mtt.gov.bh/ports-and-maritime ; http://www.mtt.gov.bh/khalifa-bin-salman-port — **PRIMARY**.
- **Mina Salman** — legacy port area (customs pier; naval use adjacent). Sea Port Customs Directorate operates at KBSP and Mina Salman. Source: http://www.customs.gov.bh/en/node/115 — **PRIMARY**.
- Posture: commercial-port waters are constraint zones (traffic separation, security), not demand nodes for the passenger network.

### 1.5 Coast Guard (Ministry of Interior)
- Licensing role: coastguard-approved transponder is a listed requirement for pleasure-boat licence renewal (iGA service description) — **SECONDARY (official iGA social channel)**; MTT small-ships process references coastguard equipment approvals — **PRIMARY** context.
- Operational control: MOI announces maritime movement restrictions (see SPEED-RULES file): night-time sea-movement bans in territorial waters announced 2025 "until further notice" — https://www.policemc.gov.bh/en/news/77453/ — **PRIMARY URL** (page unreachable at scrape time; content per search index + multiple news reposts — treat details as SECONDARY until re-pulled).
- Posture: security regulator; schedule design must absorb MOI notices (a real constraint for evening service — flagged).

### 1.6 King Fahd Causeway Authority (KFCA) — bilateral, adjacent
- Bilateral Saudi–Bahraini authority (agreement 1406 AH; Royal Decree M/9; Amiri Decree 4/1986); mandate: "Oversee the management, operation, and maintenance of King Fahd Causeway … including tolls collection." Source: https://kfca.sa/en/aboutus — **PRIMARY**.
- Relevance: causeway congestion is the flagship demand signal (see DEMAND-POOLS). Cross-border marine relief is **roadmap only** (Eastern Province market, next in sequence) — not part of the Bahrain domestic network.

### 1.7 Supreme Council for Environment (SCE)
- Confirms the Kingdom's **net-zero 2060** target announced by HRH the Crown Prince and Prime Minister at COP26; interim ~30% emissions cut by 2035. Sources: https://www.crownprince.bh/en/news/26105/ ; https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/BAHRAIN_cop26cmp16cma3_HLS_EN.pdf ; https://www.sce.gov.bh/en/… — **PRIMARY**.
- Relevance: zero-emission marine mode maps directly onto stated national targets. SCE also relevant to protected-area review for Hawar operations (see fail-closed list in NODES file).

## 2 · City/Kingdom classification
**Dual, Enable-led.** MTT/PMA is a regulator-licensor that has enabled a private operator (Masar) to run the first water taxi; government's own delivery energy is going into Metro + bus. The realistic structure is: MTT/PMA enables (licences routes, landings, vessels ≤150 GT under Decree 32/2020), Masar or a franchise operator operates, BTEA enables the visitor-economy layer, MOI/Coast Guard gates operations. Reasoning: every regulatory instrument found (registration, master licence, navigation licence, port/jetty licensing) is an MTT instrument; no evidence MTT operates vessels itself.
