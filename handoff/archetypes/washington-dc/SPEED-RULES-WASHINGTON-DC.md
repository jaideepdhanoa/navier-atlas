## 1 · The controlling instrument — found and primary-verified

The controlling District rule set is **19 DCMR Chapter 10, "Harbor and Boating Safety," § 1027 (Speed Limits)** — published by the Metropolitan Police Department as "DC Code: Harbor and Boating Safety" (source note in the text: Article 29 § 5 of the Police Regulations of the District of Columbia (January 1983), as amended by D.C. Law 11-248). Primary source (MPD-hosted PDF): https://mpdc.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/mpdc/publication/attachments/harbor_regulations_0.pdf (linked from https://mpdc.dc.gov/node/203982). Enforcement: **MPD Harbor Patrol Unit** (patrols "all of the rivers, inlets, and waterways of the District of Columbia" — https://mpdc.dc.gov/page/harbor-patrol).

Verbatim provisions (retrieved 2026-08-16):

- **§ 1027.1** — "The Mayor is authorized to establish speed limits for vessels using District of Columbia waters." (§ 1027.10 is literally "Reserved for Speed Limits set by the Mayor in accordance with § 1027.1.")
- **§ 1027.2** — outcome-based wash/wake duty: speed of all power vessels "shall be regulated to avoid danger or injury, either directly or **by the effect of the wash or wake the vessels raise** … to persons or to other vessels … piers, wharves, bulkheads, bridges, or other waterfront construction."
- **§ 1027.3** — the exemption clause: the speed limits "shall not apply … **to hydrofoil or air cushion vessels or vehicles that are operated for demonstration or experimentation purposes under the authority of a permit issued by the Mayor**."
- **§ 1027.4** — general numeric limits: no power-driven vessel over **6 statute mph** (i) in the Potomac River **upstream from Arlington Memorial Bridge**, (ii) in the **Washington Channel upstream from Hains Point**, (iii) in the **Anacostia River from Greenleaf Point to the Benning Road Bridge**; and no more than **10 mph when passing the wharf area of Alexandria, Virginia**.
- **§ 1027.8** — hydrofoil-specific: max **10 mph** between the westerly Potomac shoreline and a line 100 yards east of the pierhead line, **between Jones Point and First Street, Alexandria** (i.e., the Old Town waterfront strip).
- **§ 1027.9** — hydrofoil-specific: max **6 mph** (a) upstream of Arlington Memorial Bridge; (b) **in the Washington Channel**; (c) in the Potomac west of a line between Hunter Point and **Daingerfield Island**.

**Three consequences worth stating plainly:**
1. **DC's rules already name hydrofoils.** Unlike Boston (wash-based prohibition with a channel-permission mechanism) the DC regs regulate hydrofoils explicitly — §§ 1027.8–1027.9 cap foilborne speed in exactly the waters our launch corridors use.
2. **DC's rules also already contain a hydrofoil relief mechanism.** § 1027.3 exempts hydrofoils operated "for demonstration or experimentation purposes under the authority of a permit issued by the Mayor," and § 1027.1/§ 1027.10 give the Mayor open-ended authority to set (or reset) vessel speed limits. This is a stronger, more explicit hook than Massachusetts' marked-channel mechanism — a Navier measured-wake pilot is literally the fact pattern the exemption anticipates.
3. **The stated purpose of § 1027.2 is wash/wake harm** — the purpose a foiling N45 serves inherently. The § 1027.9 numeric caps were calibrated for 1970s–80s surface-skimming craft (the reg text groups hydrofoils with air-cushion vehicles); the relief ask is to measure us against § 1027.2's actual harm test.

## 3 · Federal overlay (internal only — never renders; hub gates ban this vocabulary on-page)

**33 CFR 165.508** (Security Zone; Potomac River and Anacostia River, Washington, DC — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/33/165.508) defines six zones spanning exactly our launch waters: Zone 1 (Potomac, Key Bridge down to a Reagan National–Hains Point line, incl. Boundary Channel and Pentagon Lagoon), Zone 2 (Anacostia below the Sousa Bridge incl. Washington Channel), Zone 3 (Potomac from the Hains Point line to the Woodrow Wilson Bridge), Zone 4 (Georgetown Channel eastern 75-yard band, enforced every July 4), Zone 5 (Boundary Channel/Pentagon Lagoon, enforced 3 fixed dates/yr: Memorial Day, Sept 11, Nov 11), Zone 6 (all of 1–3, enforced on State of the Union day).

Operationally decisive detail: **zones 1–3 are enforced only upon a notice of enforcement issued by the USCG Captain of the Port Maryland-National Capital Region** (§ 165.508(d)(1)); transit during enforcement requires COTP authorization and "minimum speed necessary to maintain a safe course." So the baseline regime is the DC harbor regs; the federal zones impose **episodic blackout/permission windows** (July 4, SOTU, three Pentagon-adjacent dates, and ad-hoc notices), not a standing speed limit. This is schedule-reliability planning input, not map content.

NPS waters/land overlay: 36 CFR Part 3 (boating in park areas) applies on NPS-administered waters; the GWMP Superintendent's Compendium (approved Dec 4, 2025) restricts vessel launching/docking to its concession marinas "or as approved by the Superintendent" (https://www.nps.gov/gwmp/learn/management/superintendent-s-compendium.htm). No NPS-set numeric speed limit on our corridors was located this pass (**unverified**).

## 4 · What relief unlocks (labeled upside only — never in base schedules)

- Base case: hub times stand — Old Town↔Wharf 43 min, Wharf↔Georgetown 22 min, Old Town↔Navy Yard 40 min, all flagged `speed_constrained`.
- The relief case is unusually concrete in DC: **§ 1027.3 (Mayoral demonstration/experimentation permit for hydrofoils) + § 1027.1 (open Mayoral authority to set vessel speed limits)** provide an in-regulation pathway to a measured-wake pilot and, ultimately, a foil-specific limit — no statutory amendment required on the face of the text. The ask to DDOT/MPD Harbor Patrol/the Mayor's office: measure wake and noise foilborne, grant a § 1027.3 demonstration permit for a defined corridor, and set foil-specific limits under § 1027.1 where § 1027.2's wash-harm purpose is demonstrably served.
- Precedent to cite: Stockholm Route 89 Candela P-12 exemption (~22 kn in a ~12 kn zone, attributed to Länsstyrelsen; formal instrument unverified) — see `../SPEED-RULE-RELIEF-PRECEDENTS.md` for the fail-closed sourcing.
- Honesty limits: § 1027.3 as written covers "demonstration or experimentation purposes" — a permanent scheduled service would need Mayoral limit-setting under § 1027.1 or a rule change; do not claim the exemption covers revenue service. Federal enforcement windows (§3 above) are independent of any DC relief. Maryland- and Virginia-water segments need their own engagement (MD DNR, VA DWR/county) — nothing verified there yet.
