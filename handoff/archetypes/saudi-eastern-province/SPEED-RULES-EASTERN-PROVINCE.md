# SPEED-RULES — Saudi Eastern Province (Arabian Gulf coast)
Internal audit file. Research date: 2026-08-16. Base schedule math at 25 kn service speed only where no posted limit constrains the leg; fail closed elsewhere.

## Headline finding
**No publicly codified numeric speed or no-wake limits were found for EP coastal waters, King Abdulaziz Port approaches, Half Moon Bay, or the Saudi side of the King Fahd Causeway.** Speed control is administrative: the Border Guard "determin[es] and identif[ies] the navigational/shipping lanes for marine boats/vessels, THEIR SPEEDS, departure and return times, and the duration of sailing" — Regulations of Security & Safety for Maritime Activities (text via Qanoniah legal DB) [PRIMARY-TEXT]. Speeds are therefore set per-permit/per-lane by Border Guard sector command (Dammam/Khobar/Qatif), not by published numeric code. This is the controlling fact for schedule design: limits will be NEGOTIATED INTO THE SAILING PERMIT.

## King Fahd Causeway — critical for any Khobar↔Bahrain roadmap line (EP-X)
- The causeway is a 25-km continuous structure from Aziziyah (south Khobar) to Al Jasra (Bahrain) on 536 pylons — https://saudipedia.com/en/king-fahd-causeway [SECONDARY]. It walls off the southern Gulf of Bahrain: a Khobar↔Manama vessel must transit a designated causeway opening.
- Bahrain-side notice: navigation beneath the causeway is confined to designated passages — Marine Notice 3/2020 "Restriction of Passing Under King Fahad [Causeway]" (listed in Bahrain MTT marine-notices register, mtt.gov.bh) [PRIMARY — register verified; PDF fetch geo-blocked; obtain full text before any EP-X schedule work]. Secondary descriptions place the permitted small-craft passage on the western span between designated columns; treat exact column numbers as UNVERIFIED until the notice PDF is in hand.
- Saudi-side marine restricted zones around Passport Island and embankments: no public text found [UNSOURCED — assume exclusion zones exist; Border Guard/KFCA consultation required].
- EP-X consequence: route must use the published passage + both countries' border formalities (see NODES; roadmap-only, no economics).
