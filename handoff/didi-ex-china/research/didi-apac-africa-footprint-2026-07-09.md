# DiDi APAC + Africa ex-China footprint audit

**As of:** 2026-07-09

## Bottom line

- **Verified direct consumer markets:** Australia, New Zealand and Egypt.
- **Current local taxi JV/partner market:** Japan (DiDi Mobility Japan, the DiDi–SoftBank JV working with taxi operators; **not** a direct private-car ride-hailing launch).
- **Overseas-travel aggregation only:** South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Cambodia, Vietnam and the Philippines. Australia, New Zealand and Egypt also appear in the current aggregation roster, but are primarily classified above as direct markets. Japan has both its local taxi-JV product and this China-app aggregation/roaming layer.
- **Confirmed historical exits:** South Africa and Kazakhstan (both 2022).
- No verified current direct DiDi operation was found in South Africa, Singapore or South Korea.

## Coverage counts and guardrails

- Country records: **14**.
- City/service-area records: **39** — Australia 28, New Zealand 3, Egypt 6 exact/current named markets, Japan 2 decision-useful waterfront confirmations.
- Australia and New Zealand city lists are complete as presented on current official pages. Egypt is deliberately partial: six exact named markets, not a guessed ten. Japan is deliberately a waterfront subset; the official page says 33 prefectures and contains a much longer granular service-area list.
- Aggregation countries have no city records because the current 1,000+ city list was not publicly enumerated in a reliable source.
