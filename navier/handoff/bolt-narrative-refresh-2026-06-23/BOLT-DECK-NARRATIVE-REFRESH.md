## 1. Saudi Arabia — **rescoped** to Jeddah + Eastern Province

**Headline:** Two dense commercial waterfront metros, both deliverable today — no giga-project timing risk.

- **The change:** NEOM, AMAALA and Red Sea Global are **out of scope**. They now appear only as an explicit "deliberately scoped away from the sovereign giga-projects" line, so the proposition is a clean, today-deliverable two-metro rollout — not a bet on resort-island ramp timing.
- **Sequence:** **Jeddah first**, then the Eastern Province.
  - *Jeddah:* Corniche ↔ Jeddah Central (PIF waterfront) ~2.4nm; Corniche ↔ Yacht Club & Marina ~12nm; Corniche ↔ Obhur marina belt ~16nm.
  - *Eastern Province:* Dammam–Khobar corniche ~10nm; Khobar ↔ Half Moon Bay ~13nm; Dammam ↔ Tarout Island ~8nm; cross-Gulf **Khobar ↔ Manama (Bahrain) ~18.6nm**.
- **Vessel:** every leg in Pioneer II range now; longer Gulf reach honestly staged on Quanta-LR.
- **The ask:** a 2-boat Pioneer II anchor on the Jeddah Corniche, then a second rollout across the Dammam–Khobar coast with the Bahrain link.

## 2. Thailand — **new** — Phuket & Phang Nga (Andaman)

**Headline:** The densest resort-and-island cluster in SEA, in a market where Bolt already operates.

- **Why now:** Bolt has run in Thailand since 2020 and is **live in Phuket** — the main challenger to Grab.
- **Use case (ridehail / leisure island-hopping):** replaces diesel speedboat day-trips with quiet zero-emission foiling.
- **Corridors (Pioneer II):** Phang Nga Bay / James Bond Island ~12nm; Koh Yao Noi (Six Senses) ~12nm; Phi Phi ~25nm; Krabi ~30nm; Similan ~50nm. Koh Samui + Langkawi staged on Quanta-LR.
- **Proof anchor:** year-round-shielded Phang Nga Bay + the marquee Yao Noi endpoint — calendar-predictable proof. 244 sealed boarding points already on the map.

## 3. Nigeria — **new** — Lagos (Lagos Lagoon)

**Headline:** The largest urban water-commuter opportunity on earth, and Bolt is #1 in the country.

- **Why now:** Bolt is **#1 in Nigeria (>60% share)**; Lagos is a ~20–25M megacity built around a lagoon where a few-km road trip takes 2–3 hours across a handful of jammed bridges.
- **Use case (commute):** CMS / Marina ↔ Victoria Island ~1.5nm; Osborne (Ikoyi) ↔ CMS ~2.5nm — short, dense, high-WTP hops that bypass the worst bridges and cross-subsidise the network.
- **Regulatory backbone exists:** **LASWA** regulates and is expanding terminals (CMS, Falomo, Ikorodu, Apapa, Badagry).
- **Proof anchor:** island CBD core first (CMS–VI–Ikoyi–Apapa), then the Ikorodu mass corridor. 59 sealed boarding points (junk-trim pending — see Grok note).

## 4. South Africa — **new** — Cape Town (Table Bay & False Bay)

**Headline:** A premium tourism + heritage anchor on one of Africa's most-visited waterfronts.

- **Why now:** Bolt is established in South Africa; the **V&A Waterfront** is one of Africa's most-visited destinations.
- **Use case (ridehail / heritage + leisure):** V&A ↔ **Robben Island** ~7nm (a constant, high-volume UNESCO heritage crossing that's diesel-ferry today); V&A ↔ Hout Bay ~12nm peninsula run.
- **Vessel:** sheltered Table Bay / False Bay corridors all inside Pioneer II range.
- **Proof anchor:** the V&A + Robben Island line — highest-footfall anchor plus a steady year-round heritage volume. 68 sealed boarding points (junk-trim pending).

## 5. Estonia — **strengthened** — Tallinn HQ & the Nordic-Baltic triangle

**Headline:** Bolt's home market — the flagship triangle, not a duplicate proposal.

- **Already existed** as `bolt-estonia`; refreshed framing only. Bolt is **headquartered in Tallinn**, which makes this the natural showcase market.
- **The triangle:** Tallinn (home anchor) → **Helsinki** (the Baltic's busiest passenger sea corridor, ~43nm, in Pioneer range) → **Stockholm** archipelago (on the Quanta-LR roadmap).
- **Local proof:** Tallinn Old City Harbour ↔ Pirita Marina ~4.5nm; Tallinn ↔ Prangli ~5nm; Viimsi / Kakumäe bay-suburb hops ~5–6nm vs long road loops.
- **Open question for Jaideep:** keep it as the combined triangle, or split out a standalone Tallinn-only proposal?
