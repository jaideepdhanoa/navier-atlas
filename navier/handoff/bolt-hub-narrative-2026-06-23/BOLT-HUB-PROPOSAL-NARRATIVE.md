## The one line

**Bolt already owns the street in Europe, the Baltics, the Gulf and much of Africa. Navier lets Bolt own the water in the same cities — the premium, congestion-beating, zero-emission layer that no super-app has claimed yet.**

## Why Bolt × Navier, now

Bolt is the largest European-founded mobility super-app — rides, scooters, food and groceries across 45+ countries — built out of **Tallinn**, with one of the deepest **African** ride-hailing footprints of any global platform. That footprint maps, market by market, onto exactly the places where the *fast path is water and nobody has digitised it*: archipelago capitals, congested coastal metros, island-hopping leisure coasts, and lagoon megacities.

Three structural unlocks make now the moment:

1. **The waterfront is unmonetised.** In almost every Bolt coastal city, water transfers are either absent, run on slow diesel ferries, or sit in cash-and-WhatsApp speedboat informality. There is no in-app, on-demand, premium layer.
2. **Navier closes the range + cost gap.** The N30 (Pioneer II, ≤70nm) covers the overwhelming majority of these hops zero-emission today; the N35 Shuttle densifies high-frequency legs at scale; the Quanta-LR hybrid (H2 2026+) extends the marquee long and cross-border legs without shore-power dependency.
3. **Bolt brings the demand, Navier brings the craft.** Distribution, payments, identity and a conditioned premium-rider base already exist in-app. The boat is the only missing primitive.

## The network shape

The Bolt × Navier opportunity is **22 sub-proposals across 8 regions**, organised as a tiered rollout — prove in the densest, most ready markets, then cascade.

| Tier | What it means | Markets |
|---|---|---|
| **Anchor** | The densest premium water-mobility proof market; first revenue + reference | **UAE** (Dubai waterfront + Dubai–Abu Dhabi + the Gulf cross-border mesh) |
| **A — flagship** | High-density, high-readiness corridors that become economics/marquee corridors | **Greece, Croatia, Italy, France-Riviera, Portugal, KSA-commercial, Qatar, Thailand (Phuket), Nigeria (Lagos), East Africa** |
| **B — expansion** | Real, mapped corridors held for the broaden-out phase | Spain, Cyprus, Egypt, Sweden (Stockholm), Finland (Helsinki), **Estonia (Tallinn triangle)**, Romania, Ireland, Lebanon, **South Africa (Cape Town)** |
| **Roll-up** | Modeled + mapped, flagged for diligence-on-demand | Israel |

### By region
- **European Med (flagship spine):** Greece, Croatia, Italy, France-Riviera, plus Spain and Cyprus — the world's densest island-hopping and premium-coast leisure markets.
- **MENA (anchor + commercial):** UAE anchor, Qatar, KSA (Jeddah + Eastern Province), Egypt, plus Israel/Lebanon held.
- **Nordic + Baltic (Bolt's home water):** the **Tallinn–Helsinki–Stockholm triangle** — three flagship Bolt cities on one Gulf-of-Finland/Baltic archipelago.
- **Atlantic Europe:** Portugal (Lisbon Tagus + Porto + Algarve) and Ireland (Dublin Bay).
- **Black Sea:** Romania (Constanța/Mamaia + Danube Delta).
- **Africa (the breakout):** **Lagos** (lagoon megacity), **Cape Town** (Table Bay / V&A), and the net-new **East Africa coastal cluster**.
- **SE Asia (bridgehead):** **Phuket / Phang Nga** — the densest resort-and-island cluster in Southeast Asia.

## What's new in this refresh

- **Estonia — the Tallinn–Helsinki–Stockholm triangle.** Tallinn is Bolt's founding city and HQ — the natural flagship for Bolt-on-water. We now frame Estonia not as a single city but as a **cross-Baltic triangle**: Tallinn, Helsinki and Stockholm are each strong Bolt markets on one connected archipelago system, giving the home-region story a marquee shape. *(Kept combined per direction — no standalone Tallinn split.)*
- **Phuket (Thailand) — SE Asia bridgehead, promoted to A.** Phuket + Phang Nga Bay is the highest-density resort/island-hop cluster in the region, today served by diesel speedboats and longtails — a flagship-grade leisure corridor and Navier's Southeast Asia foothold.
- **Lagos (Nigeria) — A.** Arguably the single largest urban water-commuter opportunity on earth: a ~20M+ megacity built around a lagoon where short crow-flies trips take 2–3 hours across gridlocked bridges. Bolt is a major Lagos rides player; the water is the unlock.
- **Cape Town (South Africa) — B.** Africa's premier coastal city around Table Bay, anchored by the V&A Waterfront and the high-volume Robben Island / Hout Bay / Simon's Town marine corridors.
- **KSA-commercial — rescoped.** Now cleanly a two-metro commercial rollout — **Jeddah (Red Sea) first → Eastern Province (Dammam/Khobar/Dhahran)** — deliberately scoped *away* from the giga-projects (NEOM, AMAALA, Red Sea Global), with a real cross-Gulf Bahrain hop.
- **East Africa — net-new coastal cluster (research-complete / seal-needed).** Kenyan coast + Tanzania mainland + the Zanzibar/Pemba/Mafia archipelago. Bolt is the **#1 rides player in Tanzania** (Africa's fastest-growing rides market, +68% YoY, 30k+ drivers across 8 cities) and was **newly licensed in Zanzibar (2025)**. Marquee **Dar es Salaam ↔ Stone Town (~41nm)** replaces the slow Azam ferry into a 917k-arrivals/yr island; **Mombasa ↔ Pemba** is modeled as a **first-class cross-border corridor** on the same footing as Dubai↔Doha (GCC) and Phuket↔Langkawi (SE Asia). Reusable for Uber/Yango and EA hospitality once sealed.

## The rollout logic

1. **Prove (Anchor + lead A):** UAE waterfront + Gulf mesh and the marquee Med corridors generate first revenue and the reference deck.
2. **Scale (A spine):** flagship Med, Qatar, KSA, Lagos, Phuket and the Dar↔Stone Town marquee become economics corridors.
3. **Broaden (B + home water):** the Baltic triangle, Atlantic Europe, Black Sea, Cape Town and the wider archipelago hops follow on proven unit economics.

Each market keeps its own corridors, boarding points, vessel gating and unit economics in its sub-proposal — the hub frames the whole; the sub-proposals carry the proof.

## The ask

A staged Bolt × Navier launch: co-develop the **UAE anchor** and **2–3 lead A corridors** into live, in-app premium water mobility, with the African breakout (Lagos, Cape Town, East Africa) and the Baltic home-water triangle sequenced behind proven economics. Navier supplies the craft, range-matched fleet and route models; Bolt supplies the demand, distribution and identity layer already in every rider's pocket.
