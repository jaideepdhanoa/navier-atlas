# Antibes
_Region: Europe_
_Last updated: 2026-06-23_
_Status: **P0 — superyacht-capital node de-bundled from Côte d'Azur; refit/charter/crew counterparty distinct from the coastal-shuttle nodes**_

## Snapshot
Antibes is the **superyacht capital of the Mediterranean** and the most consequential maritime node on the coast that the Atlas previously left as a prose footnote. **Port Vauban is Europe's largest yachting harbour — ~1,500+ berths**, including the famous **Quai des Milliardaires ("Billionaires' Quay")** with **~18–19 superyacht berths for vessels up to ~160 m**, operated by the **IYCA (International Yacht Club d'Antibes)**. Sitting at the doorstep of Cap d'Antibes, midway between Monaco and St-Tropez, the town is the Riviera's refit, charter-brokerage and **professional-crew hub** — a fundamentally different counterparty architecture (yacht industry / refit / crew logistics) from the coastal-shuttle demand at Nice or the event-surge at Cannes. That is the split-trigger: Antibes is not a leisure spoke, it is the coast's industrial-maritime anchor and deserves its own node.

## Why Navier
- **Pioneer II:** The whole Antibes network — Cannes (~6 nm), Nice (~9 nm), Monaco (~15 nm) — sits well inside the 70 nm all-electric envelope. With Europe's largest berthing inventory, the superyacht **crew-and-guest tender market** is a structural, high-frequency base distinct from seasonal leisure.
- **Quanta-LR:** Opens longer experiential and cross-coast charter positioning runs (toward St-Tropez ~40 nm, Portofino) without charging dependency.

## Demand spine
- **Port Vauban:** **Europe's largest yacht harbour, ~1,500+ berths** — the densest concentration of berthed yachts on the coast; structural tender + crew-transfer demand
- **Quai des Milliardaires (IYCA):** **~18–19 superyacht berths up to ~160 m** — the highest-value vessels on the coast, with guest/crew transfer needs to airport and neighbouring towns
- **Refit & charter cluster:** Antibes is the Riviera's yacht-industry hub (brokerage, refit yards, provisioning) — a year-round professional base, not a summer-only market
- **Professional crew hub:** the coast's largest concentration of yacht crew — recurring commute/transfer demand
- **Midpoint geography:** equidistant-ish between Monaco and St-Tropez, the natural network hinge

## Regulatory
French maritime authority (Préfecture maritime de la Méditerranée; Affaires maritimes). Port Vauban is operated under concession (Vauban 21 / IYCA); the superyacht berths are privately leased — engagement is a port-concession + club counterparty, distinct from the municipal conveners elsewhere on the coast. Low-wake all-electric foiling fits the dense-berth basin's maneuvering and environmental constraints.
