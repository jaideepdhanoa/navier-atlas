# Nice

## Snapshot
Nice is the gateway and operational anchor of the French Riviera. **Nice Côte d'Azur Airport is France's third-busiest airport** (after Paris CDG and Orly) — **~14.19M passengers in 2023, rising to ~15.23M in 2025** — and the principal port of arrival for the entire coast from Cannes to Monaco. That makes the **Nice Airport → Monaco** leg the single highest-value transfer corridor on the Riviera: today served only by helicopter (Monacair, ~7 min) or a chronically congested ~45-minute road transfer along the Basse Corniche / A8. Nice's historic harbour, **Port Lympia**, sits directly below the airport's coastal approach, and the city is the seat of the **Métropole Nice Côte d'Azur** — a single, well-resourced governance counterparty for the whole conurbation. Unlike the leisure-seasonal spokes down the coast, Nice carries a **year-round corporate and commute base** (airport transfers, business travel, second-home owners) underneath the summer peak — the demand spine that anchors a network rather than a season.

## Demand spine
- **Air gateway:** Nice Côte d'Azur Airport — **~14.19M pax (2023), ~15.23M (2025)**, France's 3rd-busiest — the arrival funnel for the whole Riviera; the onward transfer to Monaco/Cap-Ferrat/Cannes is the demand
- **Helicopter-only fast airport link:** Nice↔Monaco ~7 min by helicopter (Monacair) is the only fast link today — a sea-level foiling alternative is wide open
- **Corniche / A8 congestion:** the Basse Corniche and A8 are chronically jammed — the structural road-pain that makes water decisively faster
- **Year-round corporate base:** business travel, second-home owners and cruise transfers give Nice a non-seasonal floor the leisure spokes lack
- **Cruise & yacht anchorage:** Villefranche-sur-Mer deep-water bay and Port Lympia add tender-transfer volume

## Regulatory
French maritime authority (Préfecture maritime de la Méditerranée; Affaires maritimes) governs coastal operation; Port Lympia is managed by the **Métropole Nice Côte d'Azur / CCI**, a single well-resourced concession counterparty. Cross-border legs east of Monaco touch Italian waters. Nice's environmental positioning (low-emission zone ambitions, port electrification) favours a zero-emission foiling service.
